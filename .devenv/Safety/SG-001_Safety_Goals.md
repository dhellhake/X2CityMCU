## Goal interpretation and trace model

The **8 retained goals are top-level System Requirements with the role “safety goal”**, using the existing [requirement model](../Requirements/REQ-001_Requirements.md#requirement-types-and-architecture-model). That role introduces no fourth requirement type, hardware/software allocation or Unit Requirement. SG-001-R1.0 remains the historical released baseline; these unrenumbered retained IDs form the current Draft E/E-retrofit work product. Later safety-derived children and further allocation require controlled requirements revision.

All goals apply to `LE-EPCS`, its interfaces and the supplied pack within the scenarios and foreseeable cases identified by HARA-001. The OEM frame, running gear, steering, mechanical brakes, tray and original lock are external platform assumptions under [ID-001 §2.1](../Requirements/ID-001_Item_Definition.md#external-mechanical-platform-assumption), not project design, mitigation or qualification subjects. Regeneration remains constrained by the fitted pack's charge-acceptance condition.

**No formal ASIL is assigned** under PD CON-015. The goals address E/E causal routes, including electrical/electrochemical energy and E/E-generated heat. Each goal inherits all cited event situations and their existing qualitative risk judgments, retained assumptions and open evidence gates; combining events does not reduce their required treatment. The Draft scope change makes no new ISO-compliance or ASIL claim.

“Prevent hazardous…” states the required item-level outcome. It is not an absolute zero-risk demonstration or a command-level implementation. The functional safety concept must derive measurable torque, timing, electrical energy, E/E-generated contact temperature and visibility criteria. The candidate safe outcomes below describe what the person experiences; none selects a contactor, sensor, watchdog, algorithm or power-conversion topology.

**Trace direction:** PD/ID and a hazardous event justify a goal; a later safety requirement derives from that goal; architecture and evidence demonstrate its implementation. Existing requirements listed here **contribute intent or constrain the solution**. They are not retroactively declared fully derived from or sufficient for a safety goal. Source/provenance and derivation remain separate relations. Reverse trace is obtained by searching stable IDs in these two documents and the linked clusters.

## Safe-state interactions and timing

There is **no universal “power off = safe” state**. The concept must reconcile all affected goals and verify the transition as well as the final condition.

| Situation | Required joint reasoning |
|---|---|
| Unintended positive/negative torque | Remove the hazardous motor effect within its derived time/torque envelope ([SG-001](#sg-001), [SG-002](#sg-002)). Check the loss-of-drive/electrical-deceleration transition consequence ([SG-003](#sg-003), [SG-004](#sg-004)); a healthy command path is not assumed under output failure. |
| Regen forbidden or battery path interrupted | Protect the pack and generated-energy paths ([SG-008](#sg-008), [SG-010](#sg-010)), prevent unintended drag ([SG-002](#sg-002)), and bound electrical-deceleration withdrawal ([SG-004](#sg-004)). Continuing unsafe regenerative charge is not an acceptable means of preserving deceleration. |
| Riding fault, shutdown or exhausted usable energy | Preserve the released inhibition/restart semantics and derive physical energy and visibility behavior. Available auxiliaries do not justify harmful deep discharge, nor does supply removal establish disappearance of residual/wheel-generated energy. |
| Service / battery handling | Establish physical isolation or an explicitly controlled energized task, including wheel generation, before relying on access. A successful diagnostic or completed service task neither grants Ready nor starts a new charging session. |

**Timing derivation:** no FTTI, diagnostic interval or tolerated residual torque/energy is selected here. For each relevant event, derive the earliest hazardous outcome under the declared E/E duty and energy context; allocate sensing age, communication, diagnosis, decision, command and physical response plus uncertainty within that interval. Verify the total bound and safe-state duration/recovery. Where no dependable time-to-harm or detection is available, derive E/E prevention or electrical protection instead of inventing a detection deadline. Existing protocol periods and wire times are constraints, not safety response evidence. This extends the [interface timing budget](../Requirements/System_Requirements/Interface_Qualification.md) under HA-OI-002, HA-OI-003 and HA-OI-004.

## Compatibility with retained E/E behavior

The goals expose **unproven compatibility**, not an established contradiction or an authorization to rewrite retained E/E behavior. These are the decisions the safety concept must resolve with evidence.

| Retained choice | Consequence for safety derivation |
|---|---|

External mechanical-platform adequacy is an assumption, not a project qualification gate. Rarity of a switch-off does not close this obligation. |
| Direct Level 5 demand/resumption, maximum permitted endpoint in every level | [SG-001](#sg-001), [SG-002](#sg-002), [SG-003](#sg-003) require qualified E/E torque and transition bounds.
