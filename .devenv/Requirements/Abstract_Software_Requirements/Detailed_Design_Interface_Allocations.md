# Detailed-design interface allocations

**Draft successor — 2026-09-16; retains REQ-001-R2.0.** Retained rows keep their released bodies; the later Draft regular-DMA/Hall and fast-runtime refinements are controlled successors. These downstream Abstract Software Requirements refine released System/HSI parents and the explicit SW realization allocation in ARCH-003.

| ID | Type / target | Requirement | Derived from | Planned verification and remaining gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `REQ-SYS-INP-001` | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-INPUT-QUAL / SC-ACCELERATOR-IF.V, SC-BRAKE-IF.V, SC-TEMPERATURE-IF.V and SC-SUPPLY-IF.V | Each vehicle signal-interface software instance shall publish its supported semantic observation only with current producer/configuration/reset context, qualification and freshness, and shall represent initial, absent, invalid, stale, implausible or mismatched observations as unavailable rather than qualified semantic state. SC-TEMPERATURE-IF.V covers vehicle motor/traction-board temperature observations; battery cell/group temperature remains supplied BMS evidence interpreted by SC-BMS-LINK and SC-BAT-POLICY. | REQ-SYS-HSI-001 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: loss/stale/reset injections and each signal’s conversion/diagnostic boundary. Electrical range, sensor method, age/skew, fault envelope and response bound remain allocation/evidence gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-inp-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-INP-002` **Draft** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-INPUT-QUAL / SC-ANALOG-ACQ.V | Vehicle analog-acquisition software shall own separate regular-DMA and direct injected-JEOS completed-record stores. Each record shall carry source sequence/timestamp, producer/configuration/reset context and completion/error/overflow state; partial, overwritten, cache-incoherent, DMA-error or foreign-context records are unavailable. The regular record remains distinct from the direct fast epoch; the two units share no mutable record and the injected unit does not add scheduler or queue latency. Hall capture does not use these stores. | REQ-SYS-INP-001, REQ-SYS-HSI-009 | `T-SW`, `A`, `T-FI`: DMA completion/error/overwrite, cache attributes, reset/configuration rollover, partial scans, fast JEOS sequencing and scheduled-consumer freshness. The selected streams/rings, timing and electrical acceptance remain HSI qualification work. |
| `REQ-SYS-TRQACC-001` | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-MOTOR-CTRL / SC-TRACTION-CTRL | Traction-control software shall accept authority and signed demand only when each has current matching context, qualification and expiry evidence; otherwise it shall request zero commanded torque of both signs and publish acceptance/output-observation state separately from physical output. | REQ-SYS-HSI-004 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: producer/consumer reset, queue, expiry and invalidity. Command representation, bound, actuator contract, observation and coverage remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqpos-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQPOS-001` **Draft allocation of approved body** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-INPUT-QUAL / SC-HALL-IF.V | Hall-interface software shall transform its owned current sampled-state/edge capture and a qualified configuration map/alignment into a configuration-qualified electrical rotor-sector, direction and edge-time record. It shall withhold that record when required capture, map/alignment or context is unavailable; a static state may be accepted at rest without an edge. It shall not represent a fixed Hall truth table, mechanical angle/speed, pole count, phase alignment or control algorithm as qualified without separate evidence. | REQ-SYS-HSI-001, REQ-SYS-HSI-004 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-SYS`: static startup, capture edge sequence, stale/reset/map loss and traction consumer-withhold cases. Physical conditioning, map/calibration, timing and diagnostic coverage remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqctl-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001` **Approved** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-MOTOR-CTRL / SC-TRACTION-CTRL | Traction-control software shall realize accepted current signed wheel-torque demand through the selected Hall-sensored FOC contract: calibrated signed q-axis current reference, zero d-axis reference, bounded Hall-angle use, phase-current feedback, vector-limited PI current regulation and current-context physical-output observation. It shall withhold the realization when any required current, voltage, rotor/configuration or authority observation is unavailable. A current reference or PWM state shall not itself be represented as actual torque, electrical braking or energy direction. | REQ-SYS-HSI-001, REQ-SYS-HSI-004 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: reference signs, Hall/current/voltage/configuration loss, sample-window loss, vector saturation, rollback generating condition and output-observation distinction. | `REQ-SYS-PHASE-IF-001` **Draft** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-MOTOR-CTRL / SC-MOTOR-PHASE-IF.V | The motor-phase interface shall transform only a current raw fast epoch and accepted phase calibration into qualified same-aperture phase-current feedback, reconstruction/plausibility information and distinct regular phase-voltage diagnostics. It shall expose current health/reasons without deciding Session fault policy, and shall not substitute regular phase voltage or an earlier epoch for fast feedback. | REQ-SYS-HSI-009, REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001 | `T-SW`, `A`, `T-FI`: phase map/sign/offset/gain cases, direct-third plausibility, regular diagnostic loss, stale/foreign epoch and health/report routing. |
| `REQ-SYS-SUPPLY-IF-001` **Draft** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-INPUT-QUAL / SC-SUPPLY-IF.V | The supply interface shall transform only identified raw observations and accepted configuration/calibration into qualified fast local Vdc and separate regular supply/rail diagnostics. It shall publish current health/reasons to the applicable fast, policy and service consumers without deciding Session fault policy or substituting BMS telemetry for fast Vdc. | REQ-SYS-HSI-001, REQ-SYS-HSI-009 | `T-SW`, `A`, `T-FI`: scaling/range/context, fast/regular separation, BMS-telemetry substitution, rail diagnostic loss and health/report routing. |
| `REQ-SYS-PLT-001` | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-PLATFORM / SC-PLATFORM | Platform software shall invalidate observations, commands and acknowledgements affected by power, reset, retention or local health context change until qualified in current context, while exposing retention restore result without deciding consumer session semantics. | REQ-SYS-HSI-007 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: endpoint/rail reset and retention cases. | `REQ-SYS-PLT-CAL-001` **Draft** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-PLATFORM / SC-PLATFORM.V | Platform calibration software shall identify, validate and make available the active static register/rank/trigger configuration and calibration lifecycle context. It shall invalidate dependent interface outputs on invalid, changed or unavailable calibration, but shall not convert a current interface-health condition into a Session fault/report or write traction output. | REQ-SYS-HSI-007, REQ-SYS-HSI-009 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`: startup/reset/restore, configuration mismatch, calibration invalidation, producer-context rollover and consumer-withhold cases. |
| <a id="req-sys-plt-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-PLT-002` **Draft** | Abstract Software Requirement; LE-PLATFORM / SC-PLATFORM.V | Vehicle platform-binding software shall configure the selected startup/resource/vector/priority/scheduler bindings and route every enabled IRQ to its named project unit: regular DMA to U-ANALOG-ACQ-REGULAR, JEOS/JDR to U-ANALOG-ACQ-FAST, Hall capture to U-HALL-CAPTURE, and break/error to their direct owners. It shall integrate only generic STM32H7 driver operations and static runtime transport mechanics that meet the selected access, context, bounded-loss and priority contracts; it shall not embed vehicle pins, protocol, FOC or Session fault policy in reusable layers. It shall own static checked `Shared` access to selected fast resources and immediate fast inhibition. It publishes the current fast-inhibit reason/health condition for Session and Service classification, but SC-SESSION alone latches/reports recognized riding faults. NMI/HardFault shall have no shared-fast-resource/token access and any crash-only action shall use disjoint hardware/register ownership. A runtime/repair fast fault latches the fast safety state until the applicable clean restart; the active fast path shall prohibit unchecked access, use the required compiler-and-hardware completion barrier and bound PRIMASK masking for break-service and next-underflow qualification. | REQ-SYS-HSI-007, REQ-SYS-HSI-009 | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-HW`: vector/priority route inspection, context/reset rollout, re-entry/token lifetime, NMI/HardFault disjointness, masked-duration and barrier review, pending-break/late-commit abort, static-route overflow, health-to-Session classification and scheduler interaction. Driver/OS implementation, WCET, resource, electrical and physical-protection evidence remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqio-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQIO-001` **Draft** | `U-ANALOG-ACQ-FAST` on SC-ANALOG-ACQ.V shall be the exclusive ADC1/ADC2 JEOS/JDR raw fast-handler owner and atomically publish only completed timer-triggered raw epochs with configuration/reset and commanded-vector/ADC-context identity, channel provenance, aperture/skew and acquisition error state. SC-MOTOR-PHASE-IF.V shall derive qualified phase-current feedback from that epoch; SC-SUPPLY-IF.V shall derive qualified fast local Vdc from the same identified epoch. Each rejects unavailable, stale, partial or old-context evidence; regular diagnostic DMA records remain a distinct slower source. | REQ-SYS-HSI-009 | `T-SW`, `A`, `T-FI`: producer restart, JEOS/DMA/ADC error, cache coherency, trigger sequence, incomplete first samples, commanded-vector context rollover, conversion ownership and errata rearm sequence. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqex-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQEX-001` **Draft** | `U-TRACTION-CONTROL` shall form next compare/context data, including the next commanded-vector sampling-plan value, from one qualified current epoch; `U-TRACTION-OUTPUT` shall be its sole literal TIM8 `CCR1..4` and JSQR stage/commit writer. Together they shall atomically stage that next ADC context and PWM/trigger compares under the selected timer update guard, complete rearm and preload commit before their configured boundaries, and withdraw both torque signs for a missed deadline, invalid acquisition, break/inhibit state or configuration mismatch. They shall publish these events separately from physical-output/energy observation. | REQ-SYS-HSI-009, REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001 | `T-SW`, `A`, `T-FI`: deadline overrun, rearm/readback/guard failure, sequence loss, break, authority/Hall/configuration loss, saturation/anti-windup and compare-latch timing. |

The Unit Requirements in [Control_Unit_Requirements](../Unit_Requirements/Control_Unit_Requirements.md) derive only from these ASW records. `U-PLATFORM-RETENTION` and `U-PLATFORM-HEALTH` remain supporting units with no separate Unit Requirement.
