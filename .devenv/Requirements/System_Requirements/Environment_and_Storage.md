# Environment and storage

**Type:** System Requirement. **Targets:** `REQ-VEH-*` → [LE-VEH](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-veh); `REQ-SYS-*` → [LE-EPCS](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-epcs).

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope). Existing rider-control, startup, fault and protection rules remain controlling.

[DEC-BAT-002](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-bat-002) revises riding minimum to −10°C; outdoor storage remains −15…+40°C. [BAT-001](../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md) fixes cell discharge/charge references and estimates BMS-only storage consumption. Qualify component temperature coverage, cold-storage-to-riding transitions, actual sleep/wake loads and DEC-STO-002 normal-full entry tolerance; a cell storage rating does not qualify the complete pack.

[PD §8](../../../README.md#8-operating-environment) supplies the domain; [DEC-ENV-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-env-001) confirms both outdoor-storage configurations and retains **20 mm loose snow without underlying ice as a provisional qualification interpretation**, not an approved depth limit. Exposure profiles, traction/obstacle boundaries, local solar heating, contamination/salt, cycling and acceptance limits remain OI-055/056 derivation. PD §8.3 mechanical/EMC/ESD considerations remain upstream for later exposure and protection requirements; no test standard, ingress rating or realization is selected.

Riding ambient limits are not component or charging-temperature limits. Reduced cold/wet/snow performance is allowed only within qualified operating limits; recognized temperature faults retain [REQ-SYS-TMP-001–002](Power_Startup_and_Faults.md#temperature-limit-faults-and-common-fault-response). A protective fault response alone does not demonstrate the required environmental capability. Rating incompatibilities remain visible in engineering review under `CON-040`; this creates no new rider indication. Full dry/+20 °C reference hill/performance and RQ-001 results do not become winter guarantees.

## Storage charge budget and acceptance

[DEC-STO-002](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-sto-002) fixes both duties to normal-full entry; its conservative tolerance comes from the qualified 80% charging limit, not an arbitrary lower starting SOC. The storage floor remains independent.

For each fitted/detached duty, qualify a lower bound on full-reference capacity `Qfull,L` in Ah, entry actual SOC `z_entry,L` and the necessary storage floor `z_floor,U` as fractions. Entry must be above that floor, and the conservative charge budget must satisfy:

`Qfull,L × (z_entry,L − z_floor,U) ≥ ∫ I_all-off,upper(t) dt + Q_self-discharge,upper + U_model`.

Time is in hours and current in amperes. Include BMS modes, other residual loads, interfaces/leakage, balancing and wake/poll events once in the electrical-load integral. Self-discharge and model uncertainty are additional qualified terms. Use battery-side consumption, including paths invisible to normal terminal telemetry; neither reported 0 A nor controller switch-off proves zero draw. Nonuniform group drains and all fourteen group-voltage/temperature limits need separate coverage throughout storage; an average SOC budget is insufficient. Any energy-form budget requires the qualified voltage/SOC relationship, not nominal voltage times a fixed SOC fraction.

[BAT-001's BMS-only table](../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md#storage-load-estimate) supplies unqualified family reference bounds. Each additional continuous **1 mA** consumes **0.168 Ah over 168 h** or **0.672 Ah over 672 h**. Using the family's running rather than sleeping bound increases the budget by **1.428 Ah** or **5.712 Ah**, respectively; this compares reference bounds, not measured savings. Qualify actual mode transitions and residual consumption before crediting either mode. The documented undervoltage-related shutdown is not selected as normal storage control.

The 10% propulsion cutoff is not the storage floor. Derive all-load protection and upper-SOC/cell-temperature acceptance independently; a protective trip does not alone prove storage suitability. Inspect and establish stored condition before any post-storage charging/conditioning used for function checks, so recharge cannot hide a storage-limit breach. The empty-bay exposure case has no fitted-pack charge budget. Long connection to a powered charger remains a charging-session case; it does not acquire either unattended storage duration by analogy. DEC-STO-002 fixes entry to qualified normal full at no more than 80% actual SOC and permits normal post-storage preparation; environmental profiles, capacity/loss evidence and margins remain OI-041/056/061/063.
