The following four **approved** rows cover an EPCS function exercised by the removed battery and mobile charging equipment, outside the installed riding configuration. Pack/charger allocation and interface realization remain open. [PD §§6.7](../../../README.md#67-charging-parking-and-handling), [8.4](../../../README.md#84-charging-and-service-environment) and [11.8](../../../README.md#118-charging-battery-removal-and-service-interfaces) supply the operating context and limits.

**Normal-session behavior:** [DEC-CHG-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-chg-001) selects automatic start, ordinary resumption and completion hold until physical reconnection or qualified USB power interruption/restoration. The child requirements below elaborate [REQ-SYS-CHG-001](Battery_Handling_and_Charging.md#req-sys-chg-001). Charging fault recovery/status follows [DEC-CHG-002](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-chg-002) and the rows below; classification and realization remain open. Riding Ready, torque-inhibition and restart rules do not define off-vehicle charging recovery.

DEC-CHG-004 fixes minimum supported source capability at 9 V / 2 A. Component charging limits, profile qualification, SOC/temperature references, uncertainty, completion criteria, finite transition bounds and electrical/thermal protection remain open for the corresponding requirements/architecture gates. The 140 W limit is at USB input; conversion/auxiliary losses and charging limits determine battery power. Charge duration and the five-day work/home energy balance are OI-058 results; an eight-hour opportunity imposes no full-recharge deadline. Ordinary cold/warm waiting may continue while charging conditions remain unsuitable; eligible incomplete charging resumes automatically after the conditions clear. Thermal provisions are unselected.

## Normal charging sessions

These three **approved System Requirements** explicitly derive from [REQ-SYS-CHG-001](Battery_Handling_and_Charging.md#req-sys-chg-001), targeting `LE-EPCS`. [REQ-SYS-CHG-003](Battery_Handling_and_Charging.md#req-sys-chg-003) and [004](Battery_Handling_and_Charging.md#req-sys-chg-004) remain governing constraints, not additional inferred parents. Automatic operation never bypasses charge qualification, the 80% actual-SOC ceiling or charging-fault recovery.

**Initial charge need:** At each physical connection/reconnection or qualified USB power restoration, a battery already at or above 80% actual SOC counts as completed for that session. A later SOC decrease cannot start charging without another such event. A battery below 80% at the new-session event may wait for other charging conditions and then start automatically. This applies the owner's clarification in [DEC-CHG-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-chg-001); no separate minimum charge deficit is introduced. Numerical completion criteria, SOC uncertainty/margins, qualified USB interruption/restoration and connection events, finite response/qualification timing and operating-state continuity remain OI-041/042/049/057/061 derivation; no sensing, storage or pack/charger allocation is chosen.

The following approved contracts refine CHG-002–010. Qualification, an enable request, physical charging and visible status are distinct. An accepted source message or asserted enable alone proves neither power transfer nor protection.

For qualified positive USB voltage, verify the uncertainty- and transient-adjusted input-current upper bound against `min(I_source, I_cable, I_input-hardware, 140 W / V_USB,upper)`. No charger topology, source controller, indicator hardware or state-storage mechanism is selected.

## Charging classification and recovery acceptance

This approved classification refines CHG-004/008–010 and the [battery event matrix](Battery_Integration.md#battery-event-and-recovery-matrix). A qualified restriction is not automatically a failed component. Recognition criteria must distinguish pending qualification, bounded normal transitions and actual failure; no numerical deadline or complete diagnostic coverage is established here.

| Condition / evidence | Classification and required acceptance |
|---|---|
| Initial required information or source negotiation pending | Withhold charging; show Waiting unless a higher-priority qualified completion/fault state applies. Unknown initial SOC establishes neither charge need nor completion. Later evidence must relate to the original new-session event; expiry/failure classification requires its derived criteria. |
| Ordinary cold/healthy-too-warm waiting or expected source/cable limitation/interruption | Withhold or constrain charging as qualified conditions require; eligible incomplete sessions resume automatically under CHG-005/006. Physical source loss alone preserves the session; qualified restoration opens the new-session assessment. An uninterrupted contract change does not reopen completion. |
| Recognized loss/invalidity of information required for charging | Apply the actual charging-fault inhibition and indication. A rejected reply or pending acquisition alone is not that diagnosis; use qualified freshness/consistency and failure criteria. Valid data returning cannot clear the current-execution fault inhibition. |
| Recognized source, conversion or protective-path failure | Apply charging-fault inhibition and assigned physical protection, regardless of the selected indication or current requested charge. Expected limited source capability or deliberately disabled path alone is not a failure. BMS automatic recovery does not release the charging session. |
| Recognized forbidden charge transfer or failed withdrawal | Treat transfer beyond the qualified permitted envelope as an actual fault, including waiting/completed/inhibited states or a breached applicable cell/SOC limit. Evaluate independently measured or justified actual transfer against allowed residual charge, uncertainty and finite withdrawal bounds; neither every transient nonzero reading nor a software enable/disable bit alone establishes failure or success. |

Exercise each defined actual-fault class during charging, waiting and completed states, including an underlying fault that clears before recovery and persistent/new faults after restart. Reconnection/qualified USB restoration reassesses initial charge need; unexpected controller restart discards old fault history/indication, shows Waiting during fresh checks and retains completion/initial eligibility. BMS-only reset/telemetry recovery does neither. Verify physical withdrawal, protection and visible indication separately, including unavailable host/communication where relevant.

Temperature permission always uses the qualified charging envelope. Under [DEC-CHG-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-chg-001), a healthy battery too warm to charge but within qualified non-charging limits enters ordinary waiting; eligible incomplete charging resumes automatically after cooling when all other conditions permit. Waiting applies unless a qualified completion hold or actual fault takes priority. Cooling alone never opens a new session or clears actual-fault inhibition. Required-information faults, failed protection, applicable non-charging-limit violations and actual forbidden charge transfer retain CHG-008/009 recovery. Qualify temperature references, margins and finite withdrawal/resumption under OI-057; no new threshold, hysteresis or thermal realization is selected.
