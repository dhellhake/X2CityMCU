# Energy and protection

**Type:** System Requirement. **Targets:** `REQ-SYS-ENE-001–003` → [LE-ENERGY](../../Architecture/ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-energy); `REQ-SYS-ENE-004` → [LE-EPCS](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-epcs).

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope).

Apply [battery integration](Battery_Integration.md) to the fixed 14S5P 35E bank and selected BMS. [BAT-001](../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md) derives voltage/current and BMS-only storage references; qualify actual configured protection, UART return paths, trip/recovery and generated-energy effects. The BMS is not a selected current regulator or proof of complete independent protection.

These rows retain the PD all-load, fault, auxiliary and energy-handling boundaries. They establish obligations and planned acceptance only; they do not establish qualified limits, realized protection or passed tests. The 10% positive-propulsion cutoff is separate from all-load protection. Zero commanded torque does not imply zero passive or generated physical energy.

## Energy-state configuration and protection acceptance

This table organizes acceptance of the existing energy obligations; it creates no additional requirement, protection topology or universal fault-detection claim. Measurements establish the observed response of the selected assembly under derived cases. They do not make an unobserved condition detectable or establish that zero commanded torque eliminates stored, passive or wheel-generated energy. The 10% SOC positive-propulsion cutoff remains an operating restriction under [Battery SOC](Battery_SOC.md), separate from the all-load and energy-path protection required by `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`.

| Configuration / acceptance case | Applicable obligations | Necessary observations and acceptance focus | Implementing responsibility still to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Riding, energy paths active | `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-001`–`005`; SOC and regeneration obligations | Observe pack voltage, signed battery current, actual-SOC qualification, 14 group voltages, available temperatures, BMS protection/path state, auxiliary load, and charge/discharge response across permitted and restricted operation. Verify regulation within the qualified assembled-pack envelope; regeneration requires charge acceptance. Under `DEC-TMP-003`, valid cell temperature within the discharge envelope but outside the charge envelope permits propulsion and restricts regeneration as an ordinary restriction. | Allocate current regulation, supervisory action, BMS-path interpretation, thermal/SOC qualification and host response; qualify configured BMS settings and UART data rather than relying on family defaults or readings alone. |
| Off or faulted control with wheel rotation / generated energy | `REQ-SYS-ENE-002`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-004`–`005`; riding fault obligations | With motor command already zero, observe voltage, current, accessible contacts, dissipation/isolation path and drag/torque effects during wheel generation, protection-path removal and controller fault/off states. Verify the assigned response where the derived condition is detectable, including without HMI reporting. | Define generated-energy envelope, component/interface limits, protection response and responsible local safeguards; BMS release, controller reset or UART restoration alone cannot release a latched riding fault. |
| Connected but unseated battery during normal handling | `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-004`–`005`; [REQ-SYS-BAT-001/003](Battery_Handling_and_Charging.md#normal-battery-handling); handling obligations | Observe connector/contact state, pack and accessible-interface voltage, residual energy and auxiliary behavior through the prescribed removal/refit sequence. Confirm no commanded propulsion during normal handling; connection outside the tray does not establish Ready. | Qualify BAT-003 accessible-interface limits throughout normal handling and exposed-bay duty; derive abnormal energized-handling acceptance, residual-energy behavior and interface protection. No connection or lock sensing/interlock is selected. |
| Fitted/removed-pack storage | `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-002`–`005`; storage obligations | Apply the distinct ≥168 h fitted-outdoor and ≥672 h detached-indoor duties. Account for BMS, auxiliary/residual and self-discharge loads, DEC-STO-002 normal-full entry and available protection-path state; observe voltage/current and wake/communication effects where applicable. Storage figures from the BMS family remain references until the actual unit/configuration is qualified. | Derive storage load budget, normal-full entry tolerance, protection behavior, UART/polling impact and post-storage acceptance; do not treat the 10% propulsion cutoff as storage or all-load protection. |

Test ordinary charge-ineligible conditions, actual charging faults, restoration and controller restart. When host/UART interaction is unavailable, protection and charging inhibition must remain effective within the derived local arrangement. | Allocate charger/BMS/host roles, charge-fault classification, completion-state continuity, current/thermal limits and protection while host or UART is unavailable. BMS recovery does not itself clear a charging fault or completion hold. |
| Service, BMS disconnect or unavailable protection/power path | `REQ-SYS-ENE-002`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-003`–`005`; handling/service obligations | Observe powered, unpowered and disconnected interface states, B+ exposure, switched-negative reference changes, UART host loading and residual/generated energy at accessible interfaces. Assess response when a required protection or power path is unavailable; unavailable telemetry or protection is not assumed safely detected. | Define service isolation/handling boundaries, interface ratings, host-independent safeguards and the detectable-fault catalogue. Protection-analysis scope for deriving acceptance of the existing requirements covers the following derived cases across the configurations above: short circuit and overcurrent; weakest-group overvoltage/undervoltage; temperature excursion and invalid or unavailable sensing; switched-negative reference movement and UART/interface bypass; charger or control failure; and unavailable protection or power paths. For each case, identify the credible observation, qualified detection coverage if any, affected energy paths, protective response and local owner. `REQ-SYS-ENE-002` requires the assigned response for conditions requiring protection, including at zero torque; it does not claim every listed physical fault is detectable. `REQ-SYS-BMS-001`–`009` and `REQ-SYS-CHG-001`–`010` remain governing constraints for cell-bank limits, telemetry/path qualification and detached-charging session behavior.

## Scenario classification and response trace

This approved crosswalk routes every PD OS-001–008 and FM-001–026 to existing obligations. A physical fault may exist without being detectable; scenario membership alone is not a diagnosis or a new electronic safeguard. Required information that is unqualified cannot grant dependent permission. Recognized faults use their operating domain's response; the [riding catalogue](Power_Startup_and_Faults.md#startup-and-runtime-fault-scope), [battery matrix](Battery_Integration.md#battery-event-and-recovery-matrix) and [charging classification](Battery_Handling_and_Charging.md#charging-classification-and-recovery-acceptance) remain canonical. Hazard analysis must still derive physical protection, limits, diagnostic coverage and residual risk under OI-049.
