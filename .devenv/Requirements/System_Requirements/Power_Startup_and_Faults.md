# Power, startup and fault handling

**Type:** System Requirement. **Targets:** `REQ-SYS-*` → [LE-EPCS](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-epcs).

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope). These rules apply to every row; relocation preserves its approval/status and qualifications.

For the selected BMS, apply [battery integration](Battery_Integration.md) to telemetry qualification, actual protection events and reset domains. Vendor automatic recovery or restored communication alone cannot release existing system fault inhibition. Battery-related fault versus ordinary charge-acceptance restriction and known-temperature-violation versus unknown information retain their distinct classifications. [DEC-TMP-003](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-tmp-003) makes qualified discharge-only cell temperatures an ordinary regeneration restriction, preserving otherwise eligible propulsion. Battery startup/event criteria and reset effects are elaborated in [BMS-006–009](Battery_Integration.md#battery-qualification-and-operating-classification); the wider diagnostic coverage and numeric timing remain open.

## Startup and runtime fault scope

This approved coverage catalogue refines the existing obligations; it is not a completed hazard analysis or a claim that the listed faults are detectable. Battery cases remain in the [battery event matrix](Battery_Integration.md#battery-event-and-recovery-matrix). For each diagnostic, record the actual observation/inference, applicable startup/runtime phase, pass/fail/incomplete criteria, source/common dependencies, recognition bound and evidence. Where startup checks cannot expose a latent or load-dependent fault, assign runtime monitoring, a controlled service check or an explicit unresolved coverage gap; do not declare full coverage from a passing startup.

All recognized riding faults in the common category command both signs to zero and retain inhibition until a clean normal/unexpected restart with all Ready prerequisites. Physical inhibition and residual/generated-energy response need separate evidence under ENE-002/003. The selected first-fault report persists independently of physical clearance; reporting absence cannot suppress the response. Existing codes/ties apply after fault classification, not as a ranking of protective action.
