# Riding safety functions

**Type:** System Requirement. **Role:** functional safety / vehicle controllability. **Status:** four Draft additions for REQ-001 Draft1.7. Each row has one explicit responsible target; supporting functions and elements do not retarget the parent.

These requirements derive from the released [safety goals](../../Safety/SG-001_Safety_Goals.md) and are developed in [FC-001](../../Architecture/FC-001_Functional_Concept.md). REQ-001-R1.6 behavior remains controlling. They add physical output and interaction acceptance to the existing command, independence and profile obligations; they do not introduce new rider controls, ramps, sensors or fault-history retention. All referenced safety goals remain mandatory. Derivation is partial; a passing child alone does not satisfy a complete goal.
