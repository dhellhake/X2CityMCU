**Type:** System Requirement (Draft safety-function derivation). **Status:** Draft for `REQ-001 Draft1.7`. **Targets:** `REQ-VEH-FSC-009`, `REQ-VEH-FSC-010`, `REQ-VEH-FSC-011` → `LE-INTEGRATION` ([requirements model](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#requirement-types-and-architecture-model)).

This draft records four bounded functional obligations derived from released safety goals. It does not replace the retained-mechanical, energy, HMI/lighting, environment or service requirements, select a physical device, add a rider indication or interlock, or claim completed qualification. Existing factual-context assumptions remain assumptions until their referenced gates close. [FC-001](../../Architecture/FC-001_Functional_Concept.md) supplies the functional contracts and analysis rationale; only the explicit safety-goal column records derivation parents.

## Scope and status notes

These rows are functional safety obligations with the stated SG parents. Product performance, mass, range, storage duration, environmental capability and durability remain constraints or qualification evidence rather than new operational functions. The retained choices remain controlling: mechanical brakes require no EPCS power, regeneration has no pending/unavailable indication, pending-setting indication remains withdrawn, startup lighting is conservatively Full when required state is unknown, and fault history is not retained across restart. Any conflict found during physical derivation is to be raised through the existing decision/issue gates rather than resolved by inventing a limit or mechanism.
