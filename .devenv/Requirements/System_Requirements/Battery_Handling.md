# Battery handling

**Type:** System Requirement. **Targets:** `REQ-SYS-*` → [LE-EPCS](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-epcs); `REQ-VEH-*` → [LE-VEH](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-veh).

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope). These rules apply to every row; relocation preserves its approval/status and qualifications.

The built 14S5P 35E pack and selected JBD BMS/UART are fixed by [DEC-BAT-002](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-bat-002). [Battery integration](Battery_Integration.md) adds qualified cell charge-acceptance limits, balancing/configuration and UART protection obligations needed for riding and regeneration.

**Handling acceptance:** Map the prescribed sequence to connector, pack, lock and cover states; deliberate connection before seating must be covered. Verify touch/access protection and retained energy with physical observations, separately from no-propulsion/Ready behavior. Document inspection and recovery before reuse of known wet/contaminated, damaged, mis-mated or incompletely assembled interfaces (PD FM-017/018/024); derive credible misuse protection and its detection limits under OI-045/046/049/062. Normal riding or post-handling performance is not promised in those abnormal states. Contact construction, protective topology and any sensing remain downstream decisions.
