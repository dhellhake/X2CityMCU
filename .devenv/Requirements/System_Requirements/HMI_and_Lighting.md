# HMI indications and lighting

**Type:** System Requirement. **Targets:** `REQ-VEH-*` → [LE-VEH](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-veh).

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope). These rules apply to every row; relocation preserves its approval/status and qualifications.

The selected BMS supplies signed current and SOC candidate data through qualified UART integration ([BAT-001](../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md)). HMI conversion retains usable SOC, absolute current clamped to 51 A and invalid-current 0 A below; such fallback values cannot qualify propulsion or charging.
