# Auxiliary continuity

**Type:** System Requirement. **Target:** `LE-EPCS`. This cross-cutting obligation is allocated to the integrating EPCS because its contributors include HMIControl, LightControl and battery protection.

| ID | Requirement | Planned verification / distinguishing cases | Sources | Open dependencies / qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="req-veh-pwr-001"></a>`REQ-VEH-PWR-001` | After low-SOC propulsion stop, powered HMI/lighting shall continue while battery protection permits, under existing commands and indication rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`. L: verify auxiliary operation, retained commands, normal-off brake priority, protection boundaries and auxiliary energy use. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), `OBJ-014`, `CON-050`, `OI-063` | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-017`: No indefinite operating duration, off-state lighting or power architecture is specified. |
| <a id="req-veh-pwr-002"></a>`REQ-VEH-PWR-002` | During SOC-information fault torque inhibition, powered HMI/lighting shall continue wherever battery protection permits, under existing commands and indication rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`, `T-FI`. F/L: include SOC restoration without restart, empty fallback, retained light commands and protection no longer permitting operation. | [DEC-FLT-005](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-flt-005) | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-018`: Auxiliary permission must be established independently of failed SOC information; no forced lamp-on state. |
| <a id="req-veh-aux-001"></a>`REQ-VEH-AUX-001` | During powered-on faults, HMI and front/rear lighting shall continue wherever electrical protection and affected functions permit, preserving normal commands and brake-light trigger/priority/release rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`, `T-FI`. F/L: battery, temperature-limit/information and other faults before/after Ready; separately exercise protection requiring supply withdrawal. | [DEC-FLT-009](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-flt-009) | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-018`, `WS-OI-019`: No operation through failed display/light/supply, indefinite duration, off-state lighting or protection bypass is promised. |
