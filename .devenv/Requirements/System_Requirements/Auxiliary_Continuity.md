# Auxiliary continuity

**Type:** System Requirement. **Target:** `LE-EPCS`. This cross-cutting obligation is allocated to the integrating EPCS because its contributors include HMIControl, LightControl and battery protection.

| ID | Requirement | Planned verification / distinguishing cases | Sources | Open dependencies / qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="req-veh-pwr-001"></a>`REQ-VEH-PWR-001` | At the qualified lowest-group-voltage discharge cutoff, the system shall permit withdrawal of powered HMI, lighting, brake light and controller supply as needed to respect the qualified battery discharge-current ceiling. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`. L: verify all-load discharge bound at cutoff, including withdrawal of auxiliaries and controller power; verify voltage-based recovery under the normal startup/Ready guards if power is lost. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), `OBJ-014`, `CON-050`, `OI-063` | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-017`: Physical enforcement and bootstrap/retention remain open; no indefinite operating duration or power architecture is specified. |
| <a id="req-veh-pwr-002"></a>`REQ-VEH-PWR-002` **Draft successor; historical approval retained** | During SOC-information unavailability, powered HMI/lighting shall continue wherever independent battery protection and power availability permit, subject to any known qualified total discharge-current bound including zero, under existing commands and indication rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`, `T-FI`. F/L: include SOC restoration without restart, empty fallback, retained light commands and protection no longer permitting operation. | [DEC-FLT-005](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-flt-005) | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-018`: SOC-only loss does not inhibit torque. Auxiliary permission must be established independently of unavailable SOC information; an unqualified capability is not a known qualified voltage cutoff. Derive and verify fallback or retention consistent with this continuity obligation and any known qualified bound; no forced lamp-on state. |
| <a id="req-veh-aux-001"></a>`REQ-VEH-AUX-001` | During powered-on faults, HMI and front/rear lighting shall continue wherever power availability, independent electrical protection and affected functions permit, subject to any known qualified total discharge-current bound including zero, preserving normal commands and brake-light trigger/priority/release rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`, `T-FI`. F/L: battery, temperature-limit/information and other faults before/after Ready; separately exercise protection requiring supply withdrawal. | [DEC-FLT-009](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-flt-009) | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-018`, `WS-OI-019`: An unqualified capability is not a known qualified voltage cutoff. Derive and verify fallback or retention consistent with this continuity obligation and any known qualified bound. No operation through failed display/light/supply, indefinite duration, off-state lighting or protection bypass is promised. |
