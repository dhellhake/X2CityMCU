### Qualification evidence and response budget

| Assessment | Minimum evidence to define and obtain |
|---|---|
| Configuration | Recorded 14S5P cell bank, installed BMS/firmware, approved effective protection/capacity/balance configuration, permitted changes and evidence relating readback to that configuration. Separate commissioning evidence from checks repeated at each startup. |
| Observations | Required 14-group voltages, pack voltage, SOC, current and temperature coverage; qualified accuracy, plausible relationships, data age and source availability. Identify each consumer's mandatory subset and shared dependencies; display-only fallback grants no control permission. |
| Protection and paths | Supported protection meanings and expected charge/discharge states per configuration; distinguish reported FET command/status from proven physical transfer or isolation. Establish how detectable path/protection failures are recognized and which failures remain unobservable. |
| Time and margins | Budget source measurement age, polling/transport, qualification/detection, controller action and physical response against the shortest applicable energy/thermal/voltage margin. A fresh reply is not proof of a fresh measurement. Fast electrical protection cannot be justified solely by a slow UART supervision budget. |
| Boundary transfers | Bound residual charge after inhibition. For example, charge-induced SOC rise is `100 × I_charge,max × t / (3600 × Q_qualified,min)` percentage points; add SOC-estimation uncertainty and other applicable errors. Use qualified capacity/current bounds, not the nominal 16.75 Ah reference as measured capacity. No response time or complete margin is established by this equation. |

The allowed initialization period, measurement-age limit, missing-response/fault recognition and physical reaction budgets need numeric justification before dependent acceptance. Until then, the contract and test cases are defined but timing/coverage is not closed. Software interpretation evidence is in [BMS information](../Abstract_Software_Requirements/BMS_Information.md#selected-unit-uart-read-acceptance-contract).

This matrix applies existing policies to battery evidence; it is not a complete hazard analysis or proof that every listed failure is detectable. A recognized fault's physical response applies regardless of the selected report. All fault-group codes below use existing first-detected/tie selection and restart retention above low-priority `0x01`.

|---|---|---|---|
