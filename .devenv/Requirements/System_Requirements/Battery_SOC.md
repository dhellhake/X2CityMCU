# Battery SOC and continued auxiliary operation

**Type:** System Requirement. **Targets:** `REQ-SYS-*` → [LE-EPCS](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-epcs); `REQ-VEH-*` → [LE-VEH](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-veh).

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope). These rules apply to every row; relocation preserves its approval/status and qualifications.

[BAT-001](../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md) supplies the fixed pack and BMS source. Qualify the BMS RSOC/capacity configuration against actual SOC; neither voltage thresholds nor received percentages alone establish compliance. All thresholds and recovery rules below remain unchanged.

## Low-SOC propulsion and continued auxiliary operation

| ID | Requirement | Planned verification / distinguishing cases | Sources | Open dependencies / qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-001` | At/below 20% actual SOC, maximum permitted positive torque shall be drastically reduced to a cap determined during system validation wherever propulsion remains allowed. Abrupt entry shall be permitted in every level without a comfort ramp. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`. B/E: cross 20% with held/full/partial demand; compare reduced full-pedal endpoints across levels and respect tighter limits. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), `OBJ-014`, `CON-048`, `OI-061` | `WS-OI-003`, `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`: No pre-20% taper or cap function selected; recovery uses [REQ-SYS-SOC-008](Battery_SOC.md#req-sys-soc-008). |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-002` | At/below 20% actual SOC, maximum permitted propulsion speed shall be drastically reduced to a cap determined during system validation wherever propulsion remains allowed. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`. B: enter while moving with pedal applied, including above the new cap; no standstill/rest wait. Cover active/pending VD18MT settings above/below this cap and fixed wheel basis. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), `OBJ-014`, `CON-048`, `OI-061` | `WS-OI-001`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-015`, `WS-OI-017`: This automatic operating limit is distinct from guarded VD18MT setting changes; no automatic braking or gravity-proof speed guarantee. |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-003"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-003` | At/below 10% actual SOC, positive propulsion shall be inhibited. Abrupt withdrawal shall be permitted in every level without a comfort fade. | `I`, `T-SYS`. B: held demand through 10%, subsequent pedal changes and startup below cutoff; assess regeneration/auxiliaries independently. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), `OBJ-014`, `CON-048`, `OI-061` | `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`: 10% is a propulsion cutoff, not an all-load battery-protection floor. |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-004"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-004` | Low-SOC propulsion restriction/inhibition alone shall not inhibit otherwise permitted regeneration. | `I`, `T-SYS`. In qualifying forward travel, request regeneration in the restricted band and at/below 10%; exercise independent limits/faults. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), [DEC-REG-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-reg-001), [DEC-REG-006](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-reg-006), [DEC-SPD-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-spd-001) | `WS-OI-001`, `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-006`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`: Standstill, rollback, post-stop powered-forward qualification and Level 0 rules still apply. |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-005"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-005` | Above 20% actual SOC, low-SOC propulsion restrictions shall clear automatically under current demand and remaining permissives, without restart or extra enable action. | `I`, `T-SYS`. B: recover from both reduced capability and reached 10% cutoff; retain brake, fault, startup and other operating constraints. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001) | `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`: Normal operating recovery cannot clear fault inhibition; positive response follows [REQ-SYS-SOC-008](Battery_SOC.md#req-sys-soc-008). |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-006"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-006` | After reaching the 10% propulsion cutoff, positive torque shall remain inhibited until actual SOC exceeds 20%. | `I`, `T-SYS`. B: contrast 15% before cutoff with recovery through 12%, 15% and exactly 20% after cutoff. Stop, pedal/level changes, communication return or restart alone do not release; repeat charge cycles. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001) | `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`: Restart/battery-handling continuity remains to be derived. This operating restriction is not persistent failure history. |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-007"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-007` | At/above the active low-SOC speed cap, positive torque shall be zero regardless of demand; that cap alone shall not suppress requested regeneration. | `I`, `T-SYS`. B: enter 20% SOC already above the cap with held demand; test exact speed boundary/all levels. Accepted VD18MT/40 km/h cutoff still inhibits both signs. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001) | `WS-OI-001`, `WS-OI-003`, `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-015`, `WS-OI-017`: No automatic regeneration request is introduced. |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-008"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-008` | Positive capability restored above 20% SOC, from either reduced operation or propulsion stop, shall use [REQ-SYS-BRK-005](Brake_Interface_and_Priority.md#req-sys-brk-005) resumption ramps. | `A`, `T-SYS`. E: compare both charge-recovery cases across levels, including overlapping brake/speed/fault constraints; warning clearance is independent of ramp completion. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), [DEC-BRK-002](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-brk-002) | `WS-OI-003`, `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`: Level 0 uses Level 1's ramp while keeping its static mapping; Level 5 adds none. |
| <a id="req-sys-soc-009"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-009` | Below the active low-SOC speed cap, response to current valid positive demand shall resume automatically without pedal reset using brake-release ramps, within the reduced envelope and remaining permissives. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`. B/E: at 15% before cutoff hold demand through repeated speed crossings. Reached 10% cutoff, fault inhibition, brake or incomplete Ready must still prevent torque. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), [DEC-BRK-002](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-brk-002) | `WS-OI-001`, `WS-OI-003`, `WS-OI-005`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-017`, `WS-OI-018`: Speed-only recovery neither clears charge cutoff nor restores full capability or regenerative eligibility. |
| <a id="req-veh-pwr-001"></a>`REQ-VEH-PWR-001` | After low-SOC propulsion stop, powered HMI/lighting shall continue while battery protection permits, under existing commands and indication rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`. L: verify auxiliary operation, retained commands, normal-off brake priority, protection boundaries and auxiliary energy use. | [DEC-SOC-001](../DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-soc-001), `OBJ-014`, `CON-050`, `OI-063` | `WS-OI-007`, `WS-OI-008`, `WS-OI-010`, `WS-OI-013`, `WS-OI-016`, `WS-OI-017`: No indefinite operating duration, off-state lighting or power architecture is specified. |
| <a id="req-veh-pwr-002"></a>`REQ-VEH-PWR-002` | During SOC-information fault torque inhibition, powered HMI/lighting shall continue wherever battery protection permits, under existing commands and indication rules. | `I`, `A`, `T-SYS`, `T-FI`.
