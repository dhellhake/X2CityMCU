## Service acceptance contract

This approved elaboration of SVC-001/002 and VEH-SVC-001 defines required evidence, not a workshop procedure or access technology. The applicable service task identifies the fitted or removed-battery vehicle configuration, affected components and required power/isolation state. A documented procedure must establish accessible voltage/current/energy limits and wheel-generation controls before the corresponding task; a command, status bit or dark display does not prove isolation. Controlled energized diagnostics require their own permitted conditions.

| Acceptance subject | Required observation / rejection condition |
|---|---|
| Inspection and task entry | Cover pre-use, post-impact/abnormal-event and post-storage inspection (PD IU-019, FM-011; DEC-STO-002), normal battery handling and permitted battery/wear-part replacement. Establish stored condition before temperature conditioning or energy preparation can mask it. Define task-specific inspection/rejection and service-recovery criteria for retained mechanics, fitted/removed electrical interfaces and protective enclosures; a known unresolved critical safety anomaly prevents return to regular use (CON-025/027). Commissioning and replacement records follow the durability basis below. |
| As-built and effective configuration | Relate installed component/firmware, BMS protection/capacity settings and calibration identity to the qualified configuration; distinguish intended settings from verified effective settings. Missing or mismatched evidence cannot pass acceptance. A change requires impact review and affected verification under PD CON-022/023/026, not merely a successful write/read transaction. |
| Diagnostic meaning | Distinguish current physical observations and validity, incomplete/passed/failed checks, currently recognized fault conditions, retained session inhibition and the selected single HMI report. A hidden later fault still has its physical response. Unavailable data cannot be presented as measured zero, a passing check or proof of isolation. |
| Return to riding | Repeat the complete normal startup qualification with current inputs/settings; exercise failed/incomplete and persistent/new-fault cases. Service access, an old pass result or a cleared indication cannot grant Ready. Accepted rider settings retain their normal selection/application guards; they are not required to equal a fixed service value. |
| Observation access | Demonstrate the required current observations and identities through the documented service interface under each task's permitted power state. Missing endpoints remain explicitly unavailable. Qualify access loading, grounding, wake/poll consumption and timing effects; a diagnostic connection must not defeat required protection or functional limits. |

Configuration/calibration identity and the reached low-SOC cutoff are operational/configuration state, not historical failure records. Their required lifetimes remain distinct from restart-cleared fault history. Commissioning, replacement and executed verification evidence belongs to controlled project/service records; this does not authorize an onboard persistent fault log. Actual supplied-BMS state/history behavior remains an integration check rather than assumed compliance.

## Durability acceptance basis

Maintain one modified-vehicle commissioning origin and accumulated vehicle distance/time; replacing a battery or normal wear part does not restart the 50,000 km/five-year clock. Record installed part identity/condition, replacement point and affected verification. Assess retained donor wear independently at commissioning. The permitted replacement category is not evidence that a particular worn part remains serviceable, and it does not waive the complete-vehicle mass, fit, braking or environmental obligations.

Define a duty profile covering the specified missions, loading, starts/stops, hills, riding/storage environments and applicable calendar ageing; PD reference use does not prove an equivalent accelerated test. Qualification must relate accumulated exposure and inspection/function results to that profile and stop life accounting at the first reached target. Maintenance intervals, wear/replacement limits, test acceleration and acceptance thresholds remain OI-050 derivations. New-battery RQ-001 remains distinct from aged-battery duty; no new cycle-life or aged-range target is introduced.
