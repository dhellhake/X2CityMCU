# Accelerator signal measurement allocation

**Draft addition — 2026-09-18.** **Type:** Abstract Hardware Requirement. **Target:** [LE-ACCELERATOR-HW](../../Architecture/SystemArchitecture/ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-accelerator-hw). This requirement allocates the accelerator electrical measurement boundary without selecting a circuit, ADC/DMA arrangement, threshold, averaging method or fault-coverage claim.

| ID | Requirement | Derived from | Planned verification and remaining gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="req-sys-acc-003"></a>`REQ-SYS-ACC-003` **Draft** | The accelerator signal measurement boundary shall accept the rider electrical accelerator signal and provide an `AcceleratorMeasurement` containing the measured signal value, source identity and a measurement-integrity status with the conceptual states needed to distinguish unknown/unavailable/invalid from usable evidence. For allocated and observable failure conditions, it shall provide evidence that prevents a known-invalid or unavailable measurement from being represented as usable; this does not claim detection of every stuck, plausible or otherwise non-observable failure. Allocated measurement accuracy and response shall be derived and qualified for the complete signal boundary; no firmware timestamp, reset policy or semantic position qualification is imposed on this hardware requirement. | REQ-SYS-ACC-001, REQ-SYS-HSI-001 | `I`, `A`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: inspect signal endpoints and measurement-integrity status; inject absent, out-of-range, disconnected and allocated integrity-invalid conditions; measure accuracy and response against the derived budget; distinguish observable injected failures from unresolved/non-observable coverage and verify the software leaf cannot accept known-invalid or unavailable evidence as position. Electrical range, conditioning, diagnostic coverage and physical acceptance remain open. |
