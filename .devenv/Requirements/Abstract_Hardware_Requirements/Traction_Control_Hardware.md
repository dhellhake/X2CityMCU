# Traction-control hardware allocation

**Released in REQ-001-R2.0 — 2026-09-14. Type:** Abstract Hardware Requirement. **Target:** [LE-TRACTION-HW](../../Architecture/SystemArchitecture/ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-traction-hw), realized by HC-TRACTION-POWER with HC-INPUT-FE and HC-MOTOR interfaces. This distinct hardware allocation refines the unchanged HSI-001/004 contracts and the selected Hall-sensored FOC design; it does not select components, ratings, thresholds, pinouts, a controller board or executed hardware evidence.

| ID | Requirement | Derived from | Planned verification and remaining gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="req-sys-trqhw-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQHW-001` **Approved** | The traction hardware shall provide a complementary three-phase bridge with three phase-leg low-side current observation channels, timer-synchronous valid-sample evidence, locally observed DC-link voltage and an independently acting hardware fault shutdown path for the Hall-sensored FOC realization. It shall distinguish unavailable/saturated/out-of-window current or voltage observations from valid same-epoch feedback and shall expose physical output/energy-path observation separately from PWM/gate command. | REQ-SYS-HSI-001, REQ-SYS-HSI-004, REQ-SYS-ENE-002, REQ-SYS-ENE-003 | `I`, `A`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: phase-current polarity/scale/window/epoch, Vdc scale/latency, lost ADC/amplifier, high-modulation blind zones, desaturation/overcurrent/gate-fault response, externally driven wheel and requested-zero physical outcome. Component limits, safe fixture, numerical timing/headroom, coverage and physical acceptance remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqhw-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQHW-002` **Draft** | HC-TRACTION-POWER/HC-INPUT-FE shall realize the allocated WeAct/EVM harness, 3.3-V conditioning, external 5-V Hall supply, normally-inhibited six-channel PWM gate, and active-low independent inhibit detailed in the traction HSI. A loss of its independent supply, supervisor or fault-latch health shall force every EVM PWM input low without MCU execution and shall present a distinct inhibit observation to the MCU. | REQ-SYS-HSI-009, REQ-SYS-TRQHW-001 | `I`, `A`, `T-FI`: inspect all pins and returns; remove MCU/adapter supplies and assert each comparator/watchdog fault; measure each EVM input, BKIN indication and physical energy outcome. Component selection, thresholds, coverage and response remain qualification gates. |

The fixed topology is detailed in [MCD-001](../../Motor/MCD-001_Hall_Sensored_FOC_Technical_Design.md). The BMS UART is battery-policy/diagnostic information and cannot substitute for the local fast voltage/current paths. `PWM off`, gate-off and zero current request remain commands, not evidence of physical zero torque, zero generated energy or isolation.
