# Battery unit requirements

**Draft caller-time update — 2026-10-24; retains REQ-001-R1.9.** These are detailed-design Unit Requirements. Each has one unit target and derives only from its named Abstract Software Requirement. REQ-SYS-BMSIF-001 retains historical approval provenance but its current body/allocation is a Draft successor; active REQ-SYS-BMSIF-002–003 and REQ-SYS-BMSIF-005–014 are Draft. The links below record partial contribution, not complete coverage of those parents. System/HW constraints cited by the parent remain sources/dependencies, not parents. The units and supporting design are in [DD-002](../../Architecture/SoftwareArchitecture/DetailedDesign/DD-002_Battery_Protection_Unit_Design.md).

`U-BMS-LINK-TRANSACTION`, `U-BMS-LINK-DECODE`, `U-BMS-LINK-PUBLISH`, `U-BAT-POLICY-EVALUATE` and `U-BAT-POLICY-RESTRICTION` receive `executionTimeIn` from their immediate caller. A dispatched component or standalone unit receives a live host-boot monotonic `currentTimeMicroseconds` sample immediately before invocation; nested units inherit that bounded invocation's fixed reference and do not read a clock or resample it. The reference is not reused for another invocation. `U-BMS-LINK-PUBLISH` owns private per-field source-validity timers, checked on every invocation including those with no received frame and conservatively including remaining bounded execution to relevant use/effect. Only a newly accepted valid response for that field refreshes its timer, including an unchanged numeric value; malformed, rejected, unrelated, cached or qualification-only data does not. On field timeout, the publication unit changes only that field's qualification to unavailable; BatteryProtection then withdraws each dependent branch qualification and effective current. `U-BMS-LINK-TRANSACTION` owns UART transaction deadlines. A missed transaction deadline alone does not expire otherwise valid prior field data; startup/reset/loss of source qualification requires fresh accepted source evidence before that field requalifies. Battery values carry their qualification only, without separate freshness payload. Verify live caller sampling, fixed nested forwarding, per-field isolation, timeout, reset and queued-old-reply handling under `REQ-SYS-PLT-002`; no supplied BMS firmware obligation or invented numerical timeout is created. Timeout values and conservative source-to-use bounds remain gates derived from qualified source and end-to-end budgets.

| <a id="ur-bat-policy-001"></a>`UR-BAT-POLICY-001` | BmsLink owns private startup qualification before dependent charge/discharge output qualification at vehicle-controller startup/restart, from the two required values in the same current accepted `0x03` reply; the evaluation unit consumes resulting qualified observations/source evidence and does not implement a separate compatibility gate. No public startup Boolean or port is exposed. The evaluation unit shall consume field qualifications for its applicable operation-required input subset, implemented by `U-BMS-LINK-DECODE` under `UR-BMS-LINK-002`; BatteryProtection shall not inspect raw fields that are not published. BmsLink owns raw nominal-capacity decoding and scaling under `UR-BMS-LINK-002`; BatteryProtection's policy consumes the existing qualified `BatteryMeasurements.socSourceEvidence.reportedNominalCapacityAmpereHours` member. At normal or unexpected controller startup, `U-BAT-POLICY-EVALUATE` captures the same qualified finite positive nominal capacity C once into its single private cycle cache, shared by charge and discharge policy. It retains C for the complete controller cycle; later readings and changes in source qualification, availability, expiry, or context do not alter the cached reference. A new controller startup begins a new cycle and captures that startup’s reference; it never restores a prior-cycle value. The shared C semantics and regenerative/discharge current curves and ceiling equations are specified by REQ-SYS-BATSUP-007–015. This unit owns cache capture/lifetime and current-limit assessment; BmsLink owns raw field decode/scale. Apply the existing qualified physical-current envelope, including the 10 A ideal-charge cap and component constraints, plus path/MOS, SOC-at-90%, fault and riding restrictions. A zero resulting ceiling is known no capability only when required inputs are qualified; missing/invalid cached capacity or any required branch input leaves the dependent capability unqualified with effective current zero, and zero nominal capacity is invalid. Do not infer cell temperature from board MOS/T1; central external T2 probe assignment remains unverified. These current-capability assessments and ceilings are BatteryProtection responsibilities under U-EVALUATE/U-RESTRICTION; the evaluation unit shall require the applicable scale, group and temperature-source qualifications provided under `UR-BMS-LINK-002`. It shall qualify each charge or discharge assessment only from its applicable shared and branch-required field qualifications, all fourteen group-voltage qualifications and required protection-path assessment. Shared checks are internal and included in every dependent branch qualification; no separate common qualification is published. It shall consume BmsLink field-qualification publications through its own private BmsLink-publication-stream watchdog, checked on every invocation including when no message arrives, using caller-supplied `executionTimeIn`. A genuinely new current BmsLink publication renews delivery liveness, including when values are unchanged; cached/local rereads or republishing, and queued-old messages after a stall, do not. Startup or receiver reset begins with delivery unqualified; runtime transport loss invalidates delivery until bounded backlog resynchronization and regular live publication reestablish it. This watchdog tracks delivery liveness only and does not refresh BmsLink source-age validity. The transport contract shall conservatively bound queue/delivery/use delay; on timeout, overflow or bound violation, reject/drain or resynchronize backlog and require regular bounded live delivery before accepting qualification again. This delivery recovery does not require a new source observation if BmsLink source qualification remains current. Source timers remain private to BmsLink. The evaluation unit shall issue separate qualified effective charge-acceptance and discharge-current limits with operation-specific restriction reasons and recognized battery-fault information. Each qualified effective current limit shall equal the exact minimum of its computed pack ceiling and every other applicable qualified battery-side current ceiling or restriction; a qualified disabled/prohibited path contributes zero. A missing or unqualified required applicable constraint leaves that branch unqualified with effective zero, not omitted from selection. With no other lower qualified limit, publish the computed pack ceiling. No arbitrary additional derating is permitted; a qualified zero is known absence of capability for that operation. A qualified OFF charge path shall set only regenerative-charge current to zero; a qualified OFF discharge path shall set only discharge current to zero. OFF status alone does not establish a fault or physical isolation; recognized faults remain reported under existing rules. OFF does not invalidate an otherwise complete branch. ON removes only the corresponding path restriction and does not establish positive capability. Independently of branch current-assessment completeness, BatteryProtection shall publish `BatteryOperatingLimits.mosDisabledState` qualified from the current qualified charge- and discharge-MOS source states: `anyMosDisabled` is true when either is disabled and false only when both are enabled. Unknown, invalid or stale path source state leaves this composite status unqualified and must not be treated as either disabled or enabled; status loss follows existing BmsLink source qualification/expiry rules. Because `protectionPathStateValid` qualifies the shared protection/path record, unknown/invalid/stale path state shall leave both affected branches unqualified with zero effective current. If another required shared or branch field qualification or its publication watchdog is unavailable, every dependent branch shall be unqualified and its conservative effective limit shall be zero, without treating that zero as qualified evidence. A branch-only input loss invalidates only that branch; a required shared-input loss invalidates all branches that depend on it. BatteryOperatingLimitsAssessment carries source context and branch qualification only, not a separate common-validity, age or freshness field. Initial qualification is unknown and remains unavailable after source qualification loss until fresh accepted source evidence returns. | Per-consumer input subsets, required temperature channels, source plausibility limits and timeout criteria remain open; the regenerative and discharge current curves and ceiling formulas specified above are the stated exception. The requirements do not allocate BMS writes, vendor internals, physical energy paths, host/electrical realization, debugger tooling or other numeric thresholds.
