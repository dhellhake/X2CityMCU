# Control unit requirements

**Draft successor — 2026-09-18; retains REQ-001-R2.0.** Retained records keep their released bodies; the later Draft regular-DMA/Hall, caller-time, fast-runtime and compile-time-calibration refinements do not replace parent System verification. The calibration allocations below are Draft successors; historical release status and counts remain unchanged.

## Caller-time refinement

Every invoked unit in this table receives `executionTimeIn: ExecutionTime` from its immediate project caller. `currentTimeMicroseconds` is sampled live immediately before a dispatched component or standalone-unit invocation in the common host-boot monotonic microsecond domain and is fixed for that bounded invocation. Nested units receive their enclosing component's fixed reference; they shall not read a clock service or independently resample it, and that reference shall not be reused for a later invocation. Where its parent requires an input age, expiry or timeout decision, the unit owns that private per-input state and uses this supplied value while conservatively accounting for the maximum elapsed time through its relevant use/effect, including remaining bounded execution; for that input, initial/invalid/stale evidence is unavailable. A valid new acquisition refreshes only its own measurement timer, even if equal in value, while health-only/composite republication and queued-old-record delivery do not. Retained settings/light requests and static configuration/context keep their existing parent-defined lifecycle and are not periodically invalidated by this refinement. Hardware acquisition and edge times retain their actual occurrence time and are never rewritten on delivery. The caller-time and per-input-timeout allocation is verified through `REQ-SYS-PLT-002`; individual rows retain their parent behavior and safety intent.
