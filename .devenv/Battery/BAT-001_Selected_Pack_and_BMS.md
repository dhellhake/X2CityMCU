# BAT-001 — Selected pack and BMS

**Draft 0.1 — 2026-09-10.** Canonical project component reference; [PD §10.8](../../README.md#108-built-battery-and-selected-bms) and [DEC-BAT-002](../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-bat-002) establish selection. Manufacturer ratings, calculations and owner observations have different evidence status. No qualification test was performed for this document.

## Fixed inputs and provenance

The owner reports an already fabricated, working pack, initially fitted to the supplied BMW bay: **14 series groups of five parallel Samsung INR18650-35E cells, 70 cells total**. The owner explicitly confirmed the cell model, revised minimum riding ambient to −10°C, and selected **JBD SP14S004P14S50A**, using its **UART** for system interaction. The owner confirms the installed BMS is JBD SP14S004 hardware V1.0 with firmware 3.2. It is connected to the working pack, but its configuration remains incomplete and programmed values have not been recorded. The earlier SP14S004P14S50A designation records the selected 14S50A variant provenance; exact installed hardware and firmware are now known. Cell/topology/BMS selection and the reported initial bay fit are supplied facts; this report supplies no measured pack mass, E/E thermal or electrical protection acceptance and does not qualify mechanical bay fit or retention. Configuration values are engineering derivations to qualify, not owner-supplied decisions.

## Accepted discharge-current policy

Draft [REQ-SYS-BATSUP-012–015](../Architecture/SystemArchitecture/Modelling/BatteryControl/Decomposition/BatterySupervision.sysml) define the battery-current ceiling used for discharge capability, including its once-per-controller-cycle capacity reference, temperature/voltage minimum and qualification rules. Capability uses qualified BMS measurements directly, without numerical uncertainty compensation, as recorded in [DEC-BAT-002](../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-bat-002). The owner requires a sufficiently warm battery for propulsion: cells at or below −5°C have zero discharge ceiling, although cold-soaked controller/electronics startup remains within the −10…+40°C ambient domain. See also [OI-051](../../README.md#15-unresolved-issues).

The existing charge and discharge voltage tapers use the current assigned values in [BatterySupervision.voltageDeratingCalibration](../Architecture/SystemArchitecture/Modelling/BatteryControl/Decomposition/BatterySupervision.sysml). These assignments preserve seed-equivalent behavior and remain pending product validation; they are not new fixed normative limits or validated physical evidence. Qualification must establish strict positive finite pair ordering and correspondence to the qualified physical envelope and configuration. They are compiled component-owned parameters: any qualified change requires rebuild, deployment and restart, with no runtime update or activation path. They are vehicle policy inputs, not BMS settings or parameter writes. Manufacturer cell references 2.65 V and 4.20 V remain distinct and unchanged.

## BMS interface and protection basis

The NMC family specification describes 7–14S, 20–50 A options, common-port negative-side switching, passive balancing, one internal and one external temperature channel. Heating, charge-current regulation and secondary protection are unsupported. Board operation is specified at −20…75°C; this does not widen the cell envelope.

### Owner-reported app temperature readings (2026-10-26)

| Selected `0x03` order | App label | Owner-confirmed mapping / observation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MOS | MOS-labelled temperature; the app reading is similar to T1. The physical sensor location/role is not confirmed. |
| 2 | T1 | T1-labelled temperature; the app reading is similar to MOS. The physical sensor location/role is not confirmed. |
| 3 | T2 | Cell-pack probe placed at the pack centre; use this selected reading as `Tcell` for both current curves after field qualification. |

The owner confirms this mapping for the selected BMS: exactly three temperature values in Basic (`0x03`), ordered MOS, T1, T2. T2 is the cell-pack probe placed at pack centre; BMSIF-008 owns decoding and qualification of this selected-unit mapping, which does not claim a universal JBD protocol layout. Physical meanings of MOS and T1 remain unconfirmed. Measurement accuracy, coverage, lag, calibration, applicable thermal-trip thresholds and installed wire/schema acceptance remain open evidence. The family reference's one internal and one external channel describes family capability and does not change the selected three-value mapping.

UART: **9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, one stop bit**. J5, HY2.0-4P: **1 GND; 2 RXD; 3 TXD; 4 B+**, directions at the BMS. B+ is pack potential. Connector orientation, logic levels and ground reference require actual-unit verification. The non-isolated UART carries a manufacturer warning against energy-source/load communication. Integration must resolve that restriction without bypassing protection; a direct controller connection is not qualified.

Family example temperature trips (charge −10/65°C; discharge −20/75°C) exceed cell permissions. They are not acceptable project operating limits or evidence of actual settings. Typical balancing starts at 3.9 V with 15 mV difference, about 40 mA pulsed. Verify effective balancing without exceeding the qualified cell envelope.

## UART information contract

JBD reads use `DD A5 command length payload checksum 77`; responses use `DD command status length payload checksum 77`. The 16-bit two's-complement checksum covers command/length/payload on requests and status/length/payload on responses; multibyte values are high-byte first.

| Read | Data relevant to this project |
|---|---|
| `0x03` | Pack voltage, signed current, capacity/RSOC, version, balance/protection/FET status including charge/discharge MOS state, series count and temperatures |
| `0x04` | Series-group voltages in mV; 14 groups imply 28 payload bytes |
| `0x05` | Device/version identification string; supplement with actual configuration identity |

Voltage uses 10 mV. Current is signed, positive charging, negative discharging, normally 10 mA; V12 FET-status bit 7 selects 100 mA and corresponding 100 mAh capacity scaling instead of 10 mAh. Temperature conversion is `(raw − 2731)/10 °C`. Qualify supported lengths/extensions and scaling; translated examples contain inconsistencies. The V12 Basic (`0x03`) base field is named **nominal capacity**; a separate optional extension is named **full charge capacity**, and is not selected here. The regenerative reference `C` is the qualified BMS-reported nominal pack capacity, acquired once at each controller startup and held for the whole controller cycle. Later live capacity changes and ordinary capacity-source expiry/reinitialization do not change `C`; existing live evidence, fault and charge guards still apply. This nominal configuration/source reference is not proof of measured or aged pack capacity. Installed-unit nominal-field encoding, scaling and semantics remain qualification evidence. The `16.75 Ah` cell-derived value retains its separate provisional meaning for BMS settings and SOC/range work; it is not substituted for the runtime BMS source value.

Fourteen voltage channels observe five-cell parallel groups, not 70 individual cell conditions. The selected Basic response contains three temperature values; that does not establish complete cell/motor/inverter thermal coverage. Readback provides observations, not proof of trustworthy actual SOC or complete fault detection. The software must qualify transport framing under BMSIF-003, correlated successful transactions under BMSIF-014, and the evidence-qualified response schema, count, supported field decoding, scale and source timeliness under the applicable BMSIF requirements before dependent use; SOC bounds follow BATSUP-003, cycle capacity capture follows BATSUP-007 and its cycle retention follows BATSUP-024. The dedicated BatterySupervision startup assessment comprises exactly two checks on every normal or unexpected controller startup: firmware version **3.2** and **14 series groups**, both established from the accepted `0x03` response for that current startup. It makes no assumption that raw `0x32` is a version field or protocol revision. `0x05` identity remains supported informational data, not a startup prerequisite; physical hardware model/revision and configuration are accepted through commissioning/integration, not an additional runtime gate. Other ordinary response, decode/scaling, temperature-field/source-age, MOS-state and first/cyclic operating assessments remain applicable, but are not extra dedicated startup checks. A qualified charge or discharge MOS-disabled indication contributes the current nonlatched low-priority 0x01 warning and clears when both qualified MOS states are ON; the display's under/overvoltage wording does not prove physical cell over/undervoltage. Unknown MOS state does not invent an enabled/disabled value and existing recognized required-information fault policy applies. Actual errors reported by the BMS, including BMS-reported thermal errors, map to 0x07. Physical battery thermal protection is allocated to BMSDEV-005 and physical current protection to BMSDEV-004; BatterySupervision recognizes and publishes BMS-reported faults under BATSUP-017/020, with common report ordering under BATSUP-025. 0x06 remains for independently recognized applicable thermal-limit faults outside this battery fault path. Ordinary charge-temperature ineligibility remains a normal restriction. `lowDischargeVoltageWarning` follows the qualified lowest-group-voltage discharge cutoff under BATSUP-026; actual SOC at/below 10% alone does not set it. Its status is not a functional input or fault event under BATSUP-027. A qualified disabled charge/discharge MOS is a separate OR cause; selected fault-group reports override 0x01 regardless of order. SOC invalidity/unavailability affects SOC display/reporting only and does not by itself block Ready, inhibit qualified capability or cause a battery fault. Installed wire/schema acceptance, measurement adequacy and thermal coverage remain qualification evidence gates; installed protection/capacity settings and overall configuration acceptance remain commissioning/integration evidence. No independent physical validation is claimed. Selected polling and timing targets are specified in BatterySupervision's BMSIF/BATSUP requirements; BMSIF-013 owns cyclic polling, BMSIF-020 owns the output-publication budget, BATSUP-022 the startup deadline, and BATSUP-023 the cyclic required-field fault deadline. Installed-unit validation, source sample-age assurance and downstream physical-response budgets remain open. The protocol does not guarantee internal refresh or response bounds. No automatic parameter writing, MOS override, Bluetooth, optional history or generic heating command is selected by this UART decision.

## Consequences and remaining acceptance

- **SOC and regenerative acceptance:** use qualified temperature/group-voltage capability curves for normal current limits, including zero discharge at/below canonical `V_l` and zero charge at/above canonical `V_h` (see linked BatterySupervision calibration; subject to product validation). The 10%/90% values are longevity contexts, not SOC comparisons or independent ceilings. Low-charge 0x01 follows the qualified `V_l` boundary; actual SOC alone does not set it. Qualify BMS capacity configuration, RSOC calibration and uncertainty against actual charge evidence; a vendor full-voltage calibration procedure does not authorize vehicle operation beyond the qualified envelope.
- **Temperature:** The owner revised riding ambient to −10…+40°C, resolving the prior −15°C cell-discharge conflict. Outdoor storage retains −15…+40°C. Qualify actual cell temperatures and margins under `OI-040`/`WS-OI-019`; ambient permission alone does not grant torque after cold-soaked storage. Regeneration is cell charging and requires the charging envelope even when propulsion discharge is permissible. Existing recognized-temperature-fault inhibition/restart rules and ordinary charge-acceptance restrictions retain their distinct definitions; derive recognition boundaries, thermal margins and coverage.
- **Current and protection:** operating regulation must enforce the qualified pack envelope. The BMS is a protective backstop, not the commanded-current regulator. Validate configured thresholds, tolerances, delays, cell-group supervision and credible failure coverage; source defaults and automatic trip recovery do not satisfy vehicle fault policy. BMS recovery alone cannot clear a latched riding fault.
- **Power and UART:** assess switched-negative reference shifts, communication power/return paths and B+ exposure in riding, regeneration, shutdown, service and BMS-disconnect states. Resolve the manufacturer's UART warning through a qualified interface arrangement; do not prescribe an unverified isolation circuit. Assess generated energy when the BMS removes the charge path, and protection-limited HMI/light continuity.
- **Energy and supplied mass context:** initial bay fit is owner-confirmed as a supplied platform fact, without project mechanical fit or retention acceptance. The 3.50 kg cell mass is a calculated constituent bound; total pack mass remains unmeasured. Use the supplied 130 kg duty input for system energy/performance analysis, not as a project vehicle mass design target. Electrical contact/weather exposure and E/E thermal duty remain open. For illustration only, constant nominal voltage gives `844.2 × 0.70 = 590.94 Wh`, or `8.442 Wh/km` over 70 km before allowances. Actual `E10–80` requires a qualified curve/measurement; this illustration proves neither range success nor failure. Use the fixed pack for feasibility assessment instead of reopening cell selection.
- **Motor compatibility:** the reported, unqualified `9.5 rpm/V` gives 478.8 rpm at 50.4 V and 558.6 rpm at 58.8 V. With a merely nominal 16-inch circumference these are approximately 36.7 and 42.8 km/h at no load. The [conditional motor/energy assessment](../Requirements/System_Requirements/Vehicle_Qualification.md#fixed-pack-energy-and-motor-feasibility) gives about 55.0 V equivalent no-load reference for 40 km/h, subject to the same unqualified voltage/control convention. Loaded 40 km/h at no more than 80% actual SOC remains unresolved; neither the reported 70 V motor rating nor pack operation demonstrates it. No boost or field-weakening solution is selected (`OI-014`, `OI-018`, `OI-043`, `OI-054`).

### Storage load estimate

The BMS family lists maximum running/sleep/shutdown currents of 10 mA / 1.5 mA / 50 µA. Multiplication by duration gives the following **BMS-only** charge consumption; actual firmware/mode, other loads, self-discharge and normal-full entry tolerance remain unverified.

| Continuous mode assumption | 168 h fitted duty | 672 h detached duty |
|---|---:|---:|
| Running | 1.680 Ah | 6.720 Ah |
| Sleeping | 0.252 Ah | 1.008 Ah |
| Shutdown | 0.0084 Ah | 0.0336 Ah |

Communication can prevent sleep. Documented shutdown entry involves undervoltage and is not selected as a normal storage strategy. The 672 h dry indoor 15–30°C requirement and fitted outdoor duty use [DEC-STO-002](../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-sto-002) normal-full 80% entry. [Storage acceptance](../Requirements/System_Requirements/Environment_and_Storage.md#storage-charge-budget-and-acceptance) combines all loads, self-discharge, uncertainty and independent protection limits. Polling strategy must be assessed in that budget.

Closure is assigned to existing `OI-040/041/043/045/046/048/053/054/057/061–063` and `WS-OI-005/018/019`. Record actual unit/configuration, compatible UART electrical arrangement, captured/readback evidence and coupled qualification results before their dependent integration/acceptance gates. Use supplied vehicle mass/load context for E/E performance and energy assessment; this pack record assigns no vehicle mass ceiling or mechanical mass/retention qualification. No duplicate selection exercise or detailed internal BMS design is required.
