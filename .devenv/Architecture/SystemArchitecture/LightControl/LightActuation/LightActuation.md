# LightActuation
## Light actuation requirements

**Type:** Abstract Hardware Requirement. **Target:** `LE-LAMP-HW`. **Status:** Draft. This logical hardware composite contains LightPower (`LE-LIGHT-POWER`) and owns the lighting supply responsibility alongside physical actuation. This logical allocation selects neither a device nor a peripheral.

| ID | Allocation | Derived from | Planned verification / acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <a id="req-sys-lgthw-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-LGTHW-001` **Draft** | `LE-LAMP-HW` shall realize selected and available front On/Off and rear Off/Dim/Full logical modes as the corresponding physical illumination states, including the required rear Dim/Full distinction, subject to qualified lighting availability, supply and protection. | [LGT-001](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-001), [LGT-003](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-003), [LGT-010](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-010), [FSC-012](../LightControl.md#req-veh-fsc-012) | `T-HW`, `T-SYS`: exercise every mode and distinguish physical Dim from Full. |
| <a id="req-sys-lgthw-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-LGTHW-002` **Draft** | While powered, where no current or retained qualified unavailable restriction exists and supply and protection permit, before software can establish usable qualified mode selection after power-on or reset, `LE-LAMP-HW` shall provide front Off and rear Full. | [LGT-002](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-002), [LGT-005](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-005), [LGT-009](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-009), [FSC-012](../LightControl.md#req-veh-fsc-012) | `T-HW`, `T-SYS`: start and reset before software execution; verify front Off and rear Full. |
| <a id="req-sys-lgthw-003"></a>`REQ-SYS-LGTHW-003` **Draft** | `LE-LAMP-HW` shall retain compatibility with the released VCC/GND two-terminal rear-lamp interface. | [LGT-003](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-003), [FSC-012](../LightControl.md#req-veh-fsc-012); `DEC-LGT-001`, `CON-009`. | `I`, `T-HW`: inspect and exercise the released interface through each rear mode. |
| <a id="req-sys-lgthw-004"></a>`REQ-SYS-LGTHW-004` **Draft** | During powered faults, `LE-LAMP-HW` shall maintain requested illumination wherever lighting availability, supply, electrical protection, and affected lamp function permit. | [AUX-001](../../../../Requirements/System_Requirements/Auxiliary_Continuity.md#req-veh-aux-001), [LGT-010](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-010), [FSC-012](../LightControl.md#req-veh-fsc-012) | `T-HW`, `T-SYS`: exercise powered faults, protection withdrawal, and restoration without claiming protection bypass. |
| <a id="req-sys-lgthw-005"></a>`REQ-SYS-LGTHW-005` **Draft** | `LE-LAMP-HW`, through its contained LightPower supply responsibility, shall withdraw lighting supply or actuation when lighting availability is qualified unavailable, overriding front and rear illumination including rear Full before usable software modes exist. In the live powered actuation context it shall retain that restriction through loss of qualification until a new qualified available indication, then permit the current modes subject to supply and protection. | [LGT-010](../LightControl.md#req-veh-lgt-010), [AUX-001](../../../../Requirements/System_Requirements/Auxiliary_Continuity.md#req-veh-aux-001) | `T-HW`, `T-SYS`: verify gate during startup and all modes, qualification loss after false, and restoration with supply and protection limits. |

No PWM method, supply topology, protection threshold, brightness value, or startup timing is defined.
