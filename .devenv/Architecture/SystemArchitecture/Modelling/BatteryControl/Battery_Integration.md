# Built battery and supplied BMS integration

**Type:** System Requirement; remaining records allocated to `LE-EPCS`.

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. This file retains the approved canonical records `REQ-SYS-BMS-001`, `REQ-SYS-BMS-004` and `REQ-SYS-BMS-009`, with Draft successor bindings to `LE-EPCS`. `REQ-SYS-BMS-005` is a Draft successor to its approved baseline; its historical approval provenance is retained. The six records assigned to `LE-BATTERY-CONTROL` are canonical in [BatteryControl.sysml](BatteryControl.sysml). The binding update preserves approved normative bodies for records without changed bodies, source owner decisions and historical release; it does not claim an approved allocation release. [Model and verification rules](../../../../Requirements/REQ-001_Requirements.md) apply. These approved elaborations implement owner-confirmed [DEC-BAT-002](../../../../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-bat-002); [BAT-001](../../../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md) is the canonical component/rating reference. Selection and reported initial fit are settled, while integrated acceptance remains open.

### Qualification evidence and response budget

| Assessment | Minimum evidence to define and obtain |
|---|---|
| <a id="configuration-acceptance-baseline"></a>Configuration acceptance baseline | Controlled commissioning criteria identify the recorded 14S5P cell bank and installed BMS model, board revision and firmware; define accepted effective protection, balancing, capacity and SOC-calibration settings with units/scales, permitted changes, and qualified cell and system operating envelopes. Compare observable readback with controlled configuration evidence and retain the identity, settings and effective-behavior evidence for acceptance. The baseline is planned evidence; it is not yet qualified. Separate commissioning evidence from checks repeated at each startup. |
| <a id="per-consumer-information-acceptance"></a>Per-consumer information acceptance | Define each consumer's required-information subset and shared dependencies across 14-group voltages, pack voltage, SOC, current and available temperature coverage. Each BMS value is qualified on its own accepted response, schema/encoding/scaling, applicable single-field validity and source timeliness; disagreement between measurements is not a runtime validity check. Qualified signed pack current is consumed by RidingControl.CurrentConsumptionCoordination for shared-budget supervision as well as by HMIControl for display; field-only unavailability affects only the pack-current value's qualification and does not invalidate battery limits, status, or actual SOC. The three owner-reported app labels and provisional roles/centre-probe placement are documented in [BAT-001](../../../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md#owner-reported-app-temperature-readings-2026-10-26); temperature channels and mapping remain unverified. Derive applicable single-field physical-domain and source-timeliness criteria, including measurement-to-response latency uncertainty; publish values with qualification. Display fallback shall not qualify control information or change operating limits. |
| <a id="soc-source-acceptance"></a>SOC source acceptance | Record the accepted SOC source, calibration and capacity references, their uncertainty, and qualified SOC range and measurement age for SOC mapping, display and range information. Establish acceptance criteria for source qualification and reporting use; no calibration or accepted reference is assumed by this table. |
| Protection and paths | Supported protection meanings and expected charge/discharge states per configuration; distinguish reported FET command/status from proven physical transfer or isolation. Establish how detectable path/protection failures are recognized and which failures remain unobservable. |
| Time and margins | Budget source measurement age, polling/transport, qualification and timeout detection, qualification publication/delivery and dependent use, controller action, and physical response against the shortest applicable energy/thermal/voltage margin. BatterySupervision shall publish qualification withdrawal when a field timer expires even without new UART frames; its bounded publication and delivery delay must keep an old queued qualification from being treated as current. A newly accepted reply does not prove a newly sampled measurement. Fast electrical protection cannot be justified solely by a slow UART supervision budget. |
| Boundary transfers | Bound residual charge and energy transferred after inhibition using qualified current, voltage, temperature and response-time evidence and applicable physical operating envelopes. For example, a constant upper current bound gives `ΔQ = I_charge,max × t / 3600` Ah. This does not establish a response time or a complete physical margin. SOC calibration and uncertainty support display and range evidence, not a current-control trigger or margin; no runtime voltage or temperature uncertainty offset is prescribed here. |

Battery qualification is the current qualification of the assessed operation-required inputs and protection-path evidence represented by `batteryOperatingLimitsOut`; it is independent of effective limit magnitude. At every normal or unexpected controller startup/restart, the two checks in [REQ-SYS-BATSUP-001](Decomposition/BatterySupervision.sysml#req-sys-batsup-001) must pass from the same current accepted Basic (`0x03`) response: decoded software version 3.2 and reported series-group count 14. Until then, dependent operating limits remain unqualified with effective limits zero. Current/capacity-scale interpretation and temperature/probe schema and field qualification remain under BMSIF-005/008; fourteen group-voltage qualifications remain ongoing evidence under BMSIF-006. Frame/transaction validation, timeout/field validation, and internal shared/branch capability assessments also remain necessary, without adding startup checks or publishing a separate common-validity flag. The operation-specific required input subsets, required temperature channels, applicable individual physical-domain limits and timeout criteria remain open per consumer; pack current is consumed by CurrentConsumptionCoordination for shared-budget supervision but is not required by either BatterySupervision current-limit branch. Field-only pack-current unavailability affects only that value's qualification. A `0x05` identity read is informational, and physical hardware/model/configuration acceptance remains commissioning evidence. Loss of a required shared input withdraws each dependent branch qualification; loss of branch-only information withdraws only that branch. A qualified zero effective limit instead describes known zero capability under the current assessment. A known disabled path produces qualified zero on its own branch if the other applicable checks pass; an enabled path removes only its path restriction. Neither transition clears or establishes a RidingControl session fault latch. Loss of a Basic response or transport remains subject to independent field-age, fault-recognition and retained-fault rules. The allowed initialization period, measurement-age limit, missing-response/fault recognition and physical reaction budgets need numeric justification before dependent acceptance. Until then, the contract and test cases are defined but timing/coverage is not closed. Software interpretation evidence is in [BMS information](Decomposition/BMS_Information.md#selected-unit-uart-read-acceptance-contract).

The shared startup nominal-capacity snapshot is captured once per controller cycle and is used by both directions. [REQ-SYS-BATSUP-007–011](Decomposition/BatterySupervision.sysml#req-sys-batsup-007) define regenerative temperature/voltage ceilings; [REQ-SYS-BATSUP-012–015](Decomposition/BatterySupervision.sysml#req-sys-batsup-012) define the corresponding discharge ceiling. Both voltage tapers use the composite calibration and symbolic formulas defined by [REQ-SYS-BATSUP-016](Decomposition/BatterySupervision.sysml#req-sys-batsup-016). Capability uses the qualified reported cell-temperature reading directly and, for each direction, the maximum or minimum of the fourteen qualified group-voltage readings as specified; no numerical uncertainty compensation is applied. Each direction takes the minimum of its own temperature/voltage ceilings and qualified physical/path limits; protection and fault rules remain. SOC qualification is independent of both current branches and startup eligibility. Live SOC capacity information remains separate. Cells ≤−5°C produce zero discharge ceiling, so the owner's −10…+40°C ambient decision allows cold-soaked E/E startup but requires sufficiently warm cells for propulsion. Temperature-probe role, measurement adequacy, source age and CurrentConsumptionCoordination's pack-current supervision limits remain open; pack-current field-only absence does not affect BatterySupervision branches or SOC. See [DEC-BAT-002](../../../../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md#dec-bat-002) for the owner disposition.

A recognized fault's physical response applies regardless of the selected report. All fault-group codes below use existing first-detected/tie selection and restart retention above low-priority `0x01`.

Rows retain the complete Ready, brake, speed, profile and regenerative-application guards. A common reset spanning several vehicle controllers must satisfy each affected domain's rules; transport restoration cannot silently reset a riding session. Derive exact power/reset-domain evidence in ARCH-001 and the existing open issues.
