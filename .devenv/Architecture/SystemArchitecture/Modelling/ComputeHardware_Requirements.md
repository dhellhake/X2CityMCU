Derive/qualify mode, eligible wake input, voltage thresholds, loading, leakage, protection, clock/context restoration, wake acceptance and finite timing. |

The MCU stop/standby choice, pin allocation, thresholds and timing are open derivation inputs; this requirement selects none of them. The wake hardware must remain functional while the CPU sleeps. System integration remains subject to [REQ-SYS-STA-001](../../../Requirements/System_Requirements/Power_Startup_and_Faults.md#req-sys-sta-001)–007; wake is not a software setting receipt, startup qualification, Ready state or drive authorization. The established switch-off requirements remain in force; the pin-5 deassertion response and MCU sleep-entry mechanism remain open clarification/derivation items. When Standby is considered, review eligible wake-input sharing with existing pin assignments.
