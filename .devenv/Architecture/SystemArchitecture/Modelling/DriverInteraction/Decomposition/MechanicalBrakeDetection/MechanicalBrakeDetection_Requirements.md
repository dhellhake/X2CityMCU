# Mechanical brake detection requirements

**Draft successor allocation — 2026-09-26.** **Type:** System Requirement. **Target:** [LE-MECHANICAL-BRAKE](../../../../ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-mechanical-brake). The logical function is composed of [LE-BRAKE-SIGNAL](../../../../ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-brake-signal) and [LE-BRAKE-STATE](../../../../ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-brake-state). It refines the fixed passive two-wire brake interface without changing the retained mechanical-braking allocation or selecting an excitation, sensing circuit, numerical threshold, timing limit, diagnostic coverage, or rider proof test.

`BrakeLeverState` is the sole public brake-information composite. It has exactly `State` and `Qualification`. `State` carries `leverCombination` (`Neither`, `Left`, `Right`, or `Both`) and `measurementTimeMicroseconds`. `Qualification` carries usability, `initialQualificationPending`, and `recognizedMeasurementFault`. A state is usable only when its Qualification permits use; unavailable information does not stand for any lever combination.
