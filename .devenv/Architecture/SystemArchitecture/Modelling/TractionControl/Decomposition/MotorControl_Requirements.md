# MotorControl requirements

**Draft successor allocation — 2026-10-02.** **Type:** Abstract Software Requirement. **Target:** `LE-MOTOR-CTRL`, realized cooperatively by `SC-TRACTION-CTRL.V`, `SC-TEMPERATURE-IF.V`, and `SC-SUPPLY-IF.V`.

The rows below are the canonical successors for the transferred traction-control and temperature/supply allocations. `REQ-SYS-INP-002` remains the separate Platform-owned acquisition allocation. MotorControl decides neither Session's retained fault/report policy nor hardware shutdown; it reports qualified observations and commands actuation.

| ID | Requirement | Derived from | Planned verification and remaining gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="req-sys-inp-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-INP-001` **Draft successor allocation; relocated** | Each MotorControl temperature or supply signal-interface software instance shall publish its supported semantic observation only with current producer/configuration/reset context, qualification and age evidence, and shall represent initial, absent, invalid, stale, implausible or mismatched observations as unavailable rather than qualified semantic state. It shall use caller-supplied `executionTimeIn` for any local per-input timeout; a valid new acquisition refreshes only that measurement's timer even when its value is unchanged; health-only/unavailability publication does not refresh measurement age. Motor and traction-board temperature observations are distinct from BMS battery temperature. Fast PVDD and regular GVDD remain distinct supply observations. Motor/board-temperature and GVDD qualification shall re-run from current raw evidence independently of actuation permission or inhibition. | `REQ-SYS-HSI-001`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`, `REQ-SYS-TMP-001`, `REQ-SYS-TMP-002`. | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: live caller time, initial/invalid/stale/reset cases, unchanged valid reacquisition, health-only publication, queued old data, source identity, fast/regular separation and requalification while inhibited. Electrical ranges, sensor method, age/skew, timeout and end-to-end response remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-supply-if-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-SUPPLY-IF-001` **Draft successor allocation; relocated** | MotorControl supply-interface software shall transform only identified raw observations and immutable compiled configuration/calibration parameters into qualified fast local Vdc and separate regular supply/rail diagnostics. It shall validate those parameters during startup self-test against firmware build identity/static initialization; failed validation makes each dependent result unavailable. It shall retain current health/reasons for debugger inspection and its named policy consumers without selecting Session fault/report policy or substituting BMS telemetry for fast Vdc. | `REQ-SYS-HSI-001`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`, `REQ-SYS-TRQRAIL-001`, `REQ-SYS-TRQRAIL-002`. | `T-SW`, `A`, `T-FI`: scaling/range/context, parameter-validation failure, fast/regular separation, BMS-telemetry substitution, rail-diagnostic loss, and health/fault evidence routing. Thresholds, settling and detection budgets remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqacc-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQACC-001` **Draft successor allocation; relocated** | Traction-control software shall accept authority and signed demand only when each has current matching context, qualification and independent authority/command expiry evidence in the caller-supplied host-boot monotonic microsecond domain; otherwise it shall request zero commanded torque of both signs and publish acceptance/output-observation state separately from physical output. It shall evaluate expiry with its caller-supplied `executionTimeIn`, retain actual decision and expiry times with meaningful `*Microseconds` names, and require no paired source/policy clock identifiers or conversion metadata in this one-domain host. | `REQ-SYS-HSI-004`. | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: producer/consumer reset, queued old command, independent authority-versus-command expiry, live caller time, invalidity and recovery. Command representation, bounded invocation/expiry, actuator contract, observation and coverage remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqctl-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001` **Approved historical body; Draft successor allocation** | Traction-control software shall realize accepted current signed wheel-torque demand through the selected Hall-sensored FOC contract: calibrated signed q-axis current reference, zero d-axis reference, bounded Hall-angle use, phase-current feedback, vector-limited PI current regulation and current-context physical-output observation. It shall withhold the realization when any required current, voltage, rotor/configuration or authority observation is unavailable. A current reference or PWM state shall not itself be represented as actual torque, electrical braking or energy direction. | `REQ-SYS-HSI-001`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-004`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`. | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: reference signs, angle/current/voltage/configuration loss, sample-window loss, vector saturation, rollback generation and output-observation distinction. Calibration, timing/protection, coverage and physical acceptance remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqmotion-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQMOTION-001` **Draft successor allocation; relocated** | Traction-control software shall publish vehicle speed, travel direction and standstill only when current final MotorPosition evidence, health, context and immutable compiled motion-calibration/observability parameters support that result. It shall validate those parameters during startup self-test against firmware build identity/static initialization; failed validation makes the result unavailable. It shall perform this observation independently of Ready, demand acceptance and actuation; a static Hall state, absent edge alone, command or output-enable state is unavailable, not standstill. It shall make its result available to the existing Settings, Session and Demand guards without owning their policy. | `REQ-SYS-SPD-003`, `REQ-SYS-SPD-009`, `REQ-SYS-REG-003`, `REQ-SYS-REG-004`, `REQ-SYS-REG-009`. | `A`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: forward/reverse/standstill evidence, final-position health loss, static/absent-edge cases, parameter failure/context reset, pre-Ready observation and consumer withholding. Conversion, uncertainty/age and fixed-wheel calibration remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqact-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQACT-001` **Draft** | `LE-MOTOR-CTRL` shall issue `PhaseExcitationCommand` with `actuationInhibited` true by default and whenever authority or signed command is absent, invalid, mismatched or expired, or a required current, voltage, rotor/configuration, qualified acceptable local temperature/rail condition, or current hardware-permit condition is unavailable. It shall set `actuationInhibited` false only for a current accepted command, current required control evidence, qualified acceptable local temperature/rails, and valid `TractionHardwareProtectionEvidence` showing no active independent hardware-protection action. A zero torque command remains distinct from `actuationInhibited`. | `REQ-SYS-HSI-004`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`, `REQ-SYS-TRQACC-001`, `REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001`, `REQ-SYS-TRQRAIL-001`. | `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: initial/missing/invalid/expired authority and command; each required-evidence loss; unacceptable temperature/rail or active hardware protection; zero demand; recovery; and distinct command field outcomes. Numerical execution and response bounds remain gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-trqact-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQACT-002` **Draft** | `LE-MOTOR-CTRL` shall independently evaluate the current local startup prerequisite and recognized temperature, rail, aggregate-sensor, output-stage, and hardware-protection conditions from qualified motor/board temperature, fast PVDD, regular GVDD, supply health, aggregate sensor health, and physical stage/protection envelope evidence. It shall publish one current `TractionConditionEvidence` with `startupTractionConditionsAccepted`, `recognizedFault`, `actuationInhibited`, and applicable `conditionCategory` values to Session independently of authority/demand acceptance. It shall consume `TractionStageEvidence` only as physical-stage evidence and `TractionHardwareProtectionEvidence` only as independent hardware-protection evidence; neither command field proves physical zero output, torque, electrical braking, energy direction, or protection action. Session alone latches, reports, and determines restart lifetime. | `REQ-SYS-HSI-004`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`, `REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001`, `REQ-SYS-TRQRAIL-001`, `REQ-SYS-TRQRAIL-002`. | `I`, `T-SW`, `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: initial/startup qualification, authority/demand-independent temperature/rail/sensor/stage/protection cases, commanded inhibit, zero demand, stage-evidence loss, independent protection action and physical-output mismatch; verify current producer evidence reaches Session without a local latch. Observation accuracy and coverage remain gates. |

The qualified rail prerequisite and recognized runtime rail-fault contributions are the system rows [TRQRAIL-001](../TractionControl_Requirements.md#req-sys-trqrail-001) and [TRQRAIL-002](../TractionControl_Requirements.md#req-sys-trqrail-002). Temperature limits retain the existing `REQ-SYS-TMP-001/002` both-sign fault/restart behavior; this allocation introduces no temperature derating.
