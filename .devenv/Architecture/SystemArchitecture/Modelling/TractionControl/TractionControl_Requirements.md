# Traction control requirements

**Draft successor allocation — 2026-10-02.** **Type:** System Requirement. **Target:** `LE-TRACTION-CONTROL`, composed of `LE-MOTOR-CTRL` and `LE-TRACTION-HW`.

This successor moves the residual traction temperature and PVDD/GVDD responsibilities from the obsolete generic Input leaves into TractionControl. GVDD measurement, qualification, and operating-condition evaluation are local; only the resulting startup prerequisite and categorized condition evidence cross to Session, which retains vehicle fault latching, report selection, and restart lifetime. It does not change the approved temperature response: an applicable temperature-limit or required-temperature-information fault inhibits both signs and recovers only through the existing qualifying restart. It adds no derating behavior.

| <a id="req-sys-trqrail-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-TRQRAIL-002` **Draft** | On a recognized runtime fault of a traction rail required by the accepted operating condition, `LE-TRACTION-CONTROL` shall withdraw both torque signs and set `TractionConditionEvidence.recognizedFault` with the applicable supply category. Restoration of rail evidence, including hardware UVLO auto-recovery, shall not itself re-enable actuation; Session retains the common current-session latch and reporting policy until a qualifying clean restart. An initial unknown rail state is not this recognized fault. | Owner decision 2026-10-02; `REQ-SYS-FLT-005`, `REQ-SYS-FLT-006`, `REQ-SYS-FLT-009`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`. | `T-FI`, `T-SYS`: inject each recognized rail fault during positive, negative, and zero command; restore evidence without restart, then perform clean/persistent restart cases. Recognition criteria, response time, coverage and report selection remain gates. |

The direct Hall and phase-current/voltage observations are outside this component's logical measurement boundary. [MotorPositionEstimation](../MotorPositionEstimation/README.md) owns `LE-MOTOR-SIGNAL`; hardware can provide the shared physical realization without making TractionHardware the logical producer.
