# RidingControl low-SOC propulsion policy

**Type:** System Requirement. **Target:** `LE-RIDING`.

Part of **REQ-001-R1.6**. [Model, status and cluster index](../../../../Requirements/REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../../../../Requirements/REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../../../../Requirements/REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope). These rules apply to every row; relocation preserves its approval/status and qualifications.
[BAT-001](../../../../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md) supplies the fixed pack and BMS source. BatterySupervision qualifies actual SOC against the accepted source and calibration evidence; RidingControl applies the vehicle propulsion policy below.

The current requirement bodies are Draft successors; historical approvals remain provenance. Above 10% actual SOC no SOC-based torque or speed derate applies.

| <a id="req-sys-soc-003"></a>`REQ-SYS-SOC-003` **Draft successor** | At/below 10% actual SOC, positive propulsion shall be inhibited.
