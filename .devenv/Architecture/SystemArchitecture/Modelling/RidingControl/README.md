# RidingControl system component

`LE-RIDING` is the system responsibility for riding control. Its software-allocated leaves are `LE-RIDING-POLICY`, retaining settings, session, authority, reporting, demand and existing abstract software policy, and `LE-CURRENT-CONSUMPTION-COORDINATION`, the sole shared battery-current budget authority. Software realization remains pending. RidingControl receives battery operating limits and qualified pack current and makes them available to the coordinator; battery operating limits also support the existing Ready guards. The coordinator sends independently qualified traction budgets to riding policy for publication with the wheel-torque command. MotorControl enforces these budgets using its qualified average inverter DC-current estimate, not a second pack-current feedback loop. No dynamic auxiliary request/grant protocol is selected.

The [current coordination requirements](Decomposition/CurrentConsumptionCoordination.sysml) allocate a validated worst-case auxiliary reserve for full-bright lights, display, compute, board/conversion consumption, and justified error and transient allowances. Discharge traction budget is `max(0, battery discharge maximum − reserve)`; regenerative charge allocation does not credit auxiliary draw. Qualified pack current supports shared-budget supervision, but its absence alone does not invalidate a qualified capability or reserve-based budget. If auxiliary load alone exceeds discharge capability, zero traction cannot resolve it; existing all-load protection and auxiliary continuity policies apply. The compiled full-bright lighting component is provisionally 0.2 A battery-side equivalent for front On and rear Full over the qualified operating envelope, pending product validation; changing it requires a rebuild. Total reserve is that component plus the configuration-specific other auxiliary reserve, including nonlighting loads and common margins; the other component and complete reserve remain to be validated. Lighting availability uses this same reserve ahead of traction: qualified zero discharge capability yields qualified false, and otherwise qualified capability and a validated reserve determine availability at the inclusive threshold. Missing required inputs yield unqualified availability. Measurement error, timing, transient and supervision acceptance remain open.

RidingControl owns [resumption when the allocated discharge budget and operating guards permit](Riding_SOC_Requirements.md). BatteryControl owns directional current capability from qualified cell/group, temperature, path and other applicable limits; normal discharge uses the qualified lowest-group-voltage endpoint, with no explicit SOC threshold. SOC is used for HMI display only; BatteryStatus supplies independent low-voltage and MOS warning causes. RidingControl consumes qualified capabilities. A qualified zero capability is distinct from an unqualified assessment. The architecture allocation and external interfaces are documented in [ARCH-001](../../ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md).
