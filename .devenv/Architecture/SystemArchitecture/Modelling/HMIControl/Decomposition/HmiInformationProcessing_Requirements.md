# HMI information-processing requirements

**Type:** Abstract Software Requirement. **Target:** `LE-HMI`. **Status:** Draft allocation.

`LE-HMI` consumes the electrical endpoint's current-context transport evidence and publishes separate `ridingSettingsOut` and `runningLightStateOut` records, each containing its semantic value and qualification. It receives `SelectedFaultIndication`, qualified actual SOC/current and qualified vehicle motion to form the outgoing report. No separate HMI-health route exists; initialization or invalidity is not a received setting, and a transmission is not display acknowledgement.
