# Brake signal measurement requirements

**Draft successor allocation — 2026-09-26.** **Type:** Abstract Hardware Requirement. **Target:** [LE-BRAKE-SIGNAL](../../../ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md#le-brake-signal).

`BrakeMeasurement` is physical measurement evidence for state identification. `LE-BRAKE-SIGNAL` owns physical compatibility, conditioning/protection, and measurement responsibility at this boundary; it does not identify a lever combination or select the circuit, excitation, acquisition method, threshold, diagnostic coverage, or timing bound.

|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="req-sys-brkhw-001"></a>`REQ-SYS-BRKHW-001` **Draft** | `LE-BRAKE-SIGNAL` shall transform the fixed passive shared two-wire brake terminal interface into a `BrakeMeasurement` with an observable electrical-response value and available measurement-integrity evidence for software use. | [REQ-SYS-BRK-006](../MechanicalBrakeDetection_Requirements.md#req-sys-brk-006), [REQ-SYS-HSI-001](../../../../../Requirements/System_Requirements/System_Hardware_Software_Interface.md#req-sys-hsi-001). | `T-HW`, `I`: apply known interface conditions and nonusable evidence; verify the electrical-response value and integrity evidence are available. Electrical envelope and acquisition realization remain qualification gates. |
| <a id="req-sys-brkhw-002"></a>`REQ-SYS-BRKHW-002` **Draft** | `LE-BRAKE-SIGNAL` shall provide physical compatibility, conditioning/protection, and measurement responsibility for the fixed shared two-wire brake terminal interface over the qualified vehicle integration conditions, preserving it as one shared measurement boundary. | [REQ-SYS-BRK-006](../MechanicalBrakeDetection_Requirements.md#req-sys-brk-006), `CON-010`. | `I`, `T-HW`: inspect and test the physical boundary over the qualified integration conditions.
