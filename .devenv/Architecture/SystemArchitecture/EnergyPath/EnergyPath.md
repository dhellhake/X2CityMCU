# EnergyPath
## EnergyPath hardware allocation

**Type:** Abstract Hardware Requirement. **Target:** `LE-ENERGY-PATH`. **Status:** Draft. **Derived from:** [REQ-SYS-ENE-001](../Specification/EnergyAndProtection.sysml), [REQ-SYS-SOC-003](../BatteryControl/Specification/OperatingPolicy.sysml). This logical allocation selects no circuit or BMS command.


The requirement actuates an externally supplied bound; EnergyPath makes no SOC decision. Unqualified capability does not authorize a numerical limit, and startup/retention behavior remains a physical derivation and verification gate. Unknown capability is not a known voltage cutoff; derive fallback or retention consistent with the independent auxiliary fault-continuity obligations and any known qualified bound. This allocation is not evidence of implementation or validation.

## Energy and protection

**Targets:** `REQ-SYS-ENE-001–004` → [LE-EPCS](../../REQ-001_Requirements.md#le-epcs).

Draft successor dated **2026-10-06**; current scope is the E/E retrofit and its energy interfaces. [Model, status and cluster index](../../REQ-001_Requirements.md) · [Shared verification](../../REQ-001_Requirements.md#reading-and-verification-rules) · [Common interpretation](../../REQ-001_Requirements.md#common-interpretation-and-upstream-scope).

Apply [battery integration](../BatteryControl/BatteryControl.md) to the fixed 14S5P 35E bank and selected BMS. [BAT-001](../BatteryControl/BatteryAssembly/BatteryAssembly.md#fixed-inputs-and-provenance) derives voltage/current and BMS-only storage references; qualify actual configured protection, UART return paths, trip/recovery and generated-energy effects. The BMS is not a selected current regulator or proof of complete independent protection.

RidingControl's [CurrentConsumptionCoordination](../RidingControl/CurrentConsumptionCoordination/CurrentConsumptionCoordination.sysml) owns the single shared traction-current budget: it subtracts a validated fixed worst-case auxiliary reserve from qualified discharge capability and allocates regenerative charge no greater than charge capability. BatterySupervision pack-current qualification feeds only central excess-budget supervision; its absence alone does not invalidate other qualified battery outputs or reserve-based budgets. MotorControl applies allocations using its own qualified average inverter DC-current estimate, not pack-current feedback. If auxiliary demand alone exceeds battery discharge capability, zero traction cannot resolve it; EnergyPath must enforce the qualified discharge bound across all supplied loads, including auxiliaries; zero traction alone is insufficient.

The governing requirements retain the PD all-load, fault, auxiliary and electrical energy-handling boundaries; planned acceptance does not establish qualified limits, realized protection or passed tests. At the qualified lowest-group-voltage endpoint, normal discharge capability is zero, so the total battery discharge-current ceiling is zero; this is separate from physical protection and residual/parasitic consumption. The currently assigned endpoint is 3.10 V per group, subject to product validation, with an approximate expected relationship to around 10% actual SOC. Zero commanded torque does not imply zero stored or electrically generated energy.

### Energy-state configuration and protection acceptance

This table organizes acceptance of the existing energy obligations; it creates no additional requirement, protection topology or universal fault-detection claim. Measurements establish the observed response of the selected assembly under derived cases. They do not make an unobserved condition detectable or establish that zero commanded torque eliminates stored, passive or wheel-generated energy. The qualified lowest-group-voltage discharge-current cutoff is governed by [Battery SOC](../BatteryControl/BatteryControl.sysml) and the existing battery envelope policy `REQ-SYS-BMSPOL-001`, with EnergyPath actuation allocated by [REQ-AHW-ENEPATH-001](Specification/Requirements.sysml); physical protection remains governed by `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`.

| Configuration / acceptance case | Applicable obligations | Necessary observations and acceptance focus | Implementing responsibility still to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Riding, energy paths active | `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-001`–`005`; SOC and regeneration obligations | Observe pack voltage, signed battery current, actual-SOC qualification, 14 group voltages, available temperatures, BMS protection/path state, auxiliary load, and charge/discharge response across permitted and restricted operation. Verify regulation within the qualified assembled-pack envelope; regeneration requires charge acceptance. Under `DEC-TMP-003`, valid cell temperature within the discharge envelope but outside the charge envelope permits propulsion and restricts regeneration as an ordinary restriction. | Allocate current regulation, supervisory action, BMS-path interpretation, thermal/voltage qualification and host response; qualify configured BMS settings and UART data rather than relying on family defaults or readings alone. |
| Off or faulted control with wheel rotation / generated energy | `REQ-SYS-ENE-002`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-004`–`005`; riding fault obligations | With motor command already zero, observe voltage, current, accessible contacts, dissipation/isolation path and drag/torque effects during wheel generation, protection-path removal and controller fault/off states. Verify the assigned response where the derived condition is detectable, including without HMI reporting. | Define generated-energy envelope, component/interface limits, protection response and responsible local safeguards; BMS release, controller reset or UART restoration alone cannot release a latched riding fault. |
| Connected but unseated battery during normal handling | `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-004`–`005`; [REQ-SYS-BAT-001/003](../BatteryControl/BatteryControl.md#context-battery-handling-normal-battery-handling); handling obligations | Observe connector/contact state, pack and accessible-interface voltage, residual energy and auxiliary behavior through the prescribed removal/refit sequence. Confirm no commanded propulsion during normal handling; connection outside the tray does not establish Ready. | Qualify BAT-003 accessible-interface limits throughout normal handling and exposed-bay duty; derive abnormal energized-handling acceptance, residual-energy behavior and interface protection. No connection or lock sensing/interlock is selected. |
| Fitted/removed-pack storage | `REQ-SYS-ENE-001`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-002`–`005`; storage obligations | Apply the distinct ≥168 h fitted-outdoor and ≥672 h detached-indoor duties. Account for BMS, auxiliary/residual and self-discharge loads, DEC-STO-002 normal-full entry and available protection-path state; observe voltage/current and wake/communication effects where applicable. Storage figures from the BMS family remain references until the actual unit/configuration is qualified. | Derive storage load budget, normal-full entry tolerance, protection behavior, UART/polling impact and post-storage acceptance; do not treat the lowest-group-voltage discharge cutoff as a guarantee against parasitic or storage consumption. |
| Service, BMS disconnect or unavailable protection/power path | `REQ-SYS-ENE-002`–`003`; `REQ-SYS-BMS-003`–`005`; handling/service obligations | Observe powered, unpowered and disconnected interface states, B+ exposure, switched-negative reference changes, UART host loading and residual/generated energy at accessible interfaces. Assess response when a required protection or power path is unavailable; unavailable telemetry or protection is not assumed safely detected. | Define service isolation/handling boundaries, interface ratings, host-independent safeguards and the detectable-fault catalogue. Preserve BMS protection; UART wiring must not bypass switching or energize incompatible circuitry. |

Protection-analysis scope for deriving acceptance of the existing requirements covers the following derived cases across the configurations above: short circuit and overcurrent; weakest-group overvoltage/undervoltage; temperature excursion and invalid or unavailable sensing; switched-negative reference movement and UART/interface bypass; vehicle control failure; and unavailable protection or power paths. For each case, identify the credible observation, qualified detection coverage if any, affected energy paths, protective response and local owner. `REQ-SYS-ENE-002` requires the assigned response for conditions requiring protection, including at zero torque; it does not claim every listed physical fault is detectable. `REQ-SYS-BMS-001`–`009` remain governing constraints for cell-bank limits and telemetry/path qualification in the vehicle project.

## Energy safety functions

**Status:** Draft additions for `REQ-001 Draft1.7`; supporting System Requirements remain released in `REQ-001-R1.6` and safety-goal parents in [SG-001-R1.0](../../../Safety/SG-001_Safety_Goals.md). **Concept target:** [FC-001](../../FC-001_Functional_Concept.md).

These requirements derive person- and vehicle-level energy outcomes from the released safety goals.  They do not select a sensing method, protection topology, host, numerical threshold, fault-detection coverage, or a claim that every physical fault is electronically detectable.  The declared credible fault envelope must receive an assigned preventive, limiting, containment, isolation, or recovery treatment even where direct recognition is unavailable.  Existing requirements cited as sources constrain the solution; they are not automatically additional derivation parents.


The SysML requirements define functional outcomes and evidence gates. They do not amend vehicle SOC policy, regenerative charge-acceptance rules, the BMS data contract or the normal handling sequence, and they do not imply that a reset retains historical failures beyond the released rules.
