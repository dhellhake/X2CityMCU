# X2CityMCU system architecture SysML v2 model

`ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md` is the logical EPCS architecture authority. The [X2CityMCU root model](Modelling/X2CityMCU/X2CityMCU.sysml) represents `X2CityMCU` (`LE-EPCS`); its folder also contains shared contracts and the EnergyPath child model. Load the `.sysml` resources in `Modelling/` recursively, including subfolders, as one SysML v2 model, rooted at `X2CitySystemArchitecture`.

Component navigation: [Traction](Modelling/Traction/Traction.sysml), [Motor position estimation](Modelling/Traction/Decomposition/MotorPositionEstimation/README.md), [Traction control](Modelling/Traction/Decomposition/TractionControl/README.md), [DriverInteraction](Modelling/DriverInteraction/DriverInteraction.sysml) ([requirements](Modelling/DriverInteraction/DriverInteraction_Requirements.md)), [Accelerator position identification](Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/AcceleratorPositionIdentification/README.md), [Mechanical brake detection](Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/MechanicalBrakeDetection/README.md), [HMIControl](Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/HMIControl/README.md), [Compute](Modelling/Compute/README.md), [LightControl](Modelling/LightControl/README.md), [RidingControl](Modelling/RidingControl/README.md), and [BatteryControl](Modelling/BatteryControl/README.md).

The model is static and logical. It defines the electrical retrofit composition and information contracts `IF-A-001` through `IF-A-011`; [ARCH-002](../ARCH-002_Hardware_Architecture.md) owns conductors, pins, ground, voltage classes and connector realization. `LE-COMPUTE` composes only `LE-COMPUTE-HW` and `LE-PLATFORM` and hosts the selected WeAct STM32H723VGT6 EPCS execution infrastructure; system-function ownership remains separate from hosting. HMIControl owns the physical power-lock observation and qualified On/Off request interpretation; it publishes the operation record to RidingControl only. Its separately conditioned hardware wake reaches ComputeHardware while software sleeps. RidingControl is a System composite of `LE-RIDING-POLICY` and `LE-CURRENT-CONSUMPTION-COORDINATION`. The policy leaf owns riding/session behavior; the coordination leaf owns one central battery-current budget, using a validated fixed worst-case auxiliary reserve and qualified signed pack current only for shared-budget supervision. The same qualified discharge capability and full auxiliary reserve (including lighting exactly once) produce qualified lighting availability; the status routes from RidingControl to LightControl and then LightActuation/LightPower. Qualified unavailable status overrides all lamp modes and the rear Full startup fallback and remains restrictive through input unqualification until qualified available status returns; initially unknown status retains existing fallback behavior within power/protection limits. The immutable front-On plus rear-Full lighting calibration remains provisional pending product validation; other auxiliary reserve validation is open. MotorControl enforces allocated traction budgets using its own qualified average inverter DC-current estimate, without a BMS pack-current input. Loss of BMS pack current alone does not invalidate other BatterySupervision outputs or reserve-based budgets. Auxiliary-only demand beyond discharge capability cannot be resolved by zero traction and remains under existing all-load protection and continuity obligations. RidingPolicy evaluates sleep eligibility from qualified explicit Off and traction `computeSleepPermitted` capability evidence, then grants or revokes the request; Platform validates the current request at actual entry and executes it. EnergyPath supply, runtime readiness and guarded sleep exchanges are summarized in ARCH-001. See the [HMIControl overview](Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/HMIControl/README.md) for operation qualification and the [Compute overview](Modelling/Compute/README.md) for startup and sleep sequencing. `DriverInteraction` (`LE-INPUT`) contains accelerator, coded-brake and HMIControl interaction. Its `driverElectricalConnection` groups their distinct typed electrical subinterfaces, and its semantic ports route qualified outputs, display power and hardware wake through the parent. `LE-TRACTION` contains sibling `LE-TRACTION-CONTROL` and `LE-MOTOR-POSITION`. TractionControl composes `LE-MOTOR-CTRL` and `LE-TRACTION-HW`: MotorControl qualifies local motor/board temperature and PVDD/GVDD evidence, commands typed phase excitation, and consumes independently observed physical-stage/protection evidence. Qualified GVDD and its operating-condition evaluation remain within TractionControl; RidingPolicy receives startup prerequisite and categorized condition evidence for vehicle-level latching/reporting/restart policy. TractionHardware retains independent actuation inhibition and physical motor/energy boundaries. Hall and phase-current/voltage inputs go directly to MotorPositionEstimation and never through TractionHardware; the estimator alone provides the complete `MotorPosition` composite to MotorControl, Platform and RidingPolicy. A physical board can contribute to several of these logical functions without changing their ownership. Software realization of these logical responsibilities is pending. The other EPCS composition and boundaries remain as described in ARCH-001.

Validation is structural and source-contract review only; this Draft does not claim a SysML compiler result.
