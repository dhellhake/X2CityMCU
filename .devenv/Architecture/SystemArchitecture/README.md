# X2City vehicle system architecture SysML v2 model

`ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md` is the logical vehicle architecture authority. Load the `.sysml` resources in `Modelling/` recursively, including subfolders, as one SysML v2 model, rooted at `X2CitySystemArchitecture`.

Component navigation: [Accelerator position identification](Modelling/AcceleratorPositionIdentification/README.md) (parent decomposition, hardware signal measurement, and software position processing) and [LightControl](Modelling/LightControl/README.md) (mode selection and light actuation), and [BatteryControl](Modelling/BatteryControl/README.md) (BatteryAssembly, BatterySupervision and required battery contracts).

The model is static and logical. It defines vehicle component composition and information contracts `IF-A-001` through `IF-A-006`, `IF-A-009` and the physical-energy boundary `IF-A-010`. `LE-ACCELERATOR` is the Draft end-to-end accelerator function: RiderDevices electrical signal through logical signal measurement and position processing to one qualified `AcceleratorPosition`, with typed policy and platform-health routes. The two leaves describe HW/SW responsibility; acquisition and transfer realization remains prospective. `LE-LIGHT-CONTROL` receives the qualified normal request, mechanical brake-lever state, actual electrical-braking state and external `vehicleElectricalPowerIn`; its `LightModeSelection` and `LightActuation` children use the Front Off/On and Rear Off/Dim/Full contract. `LightActuation` contains `LightPower`, which provides `lightingPowerOut`; `LE-ENERGY-PATH` distributes `vehicleElectricalPowerOut` into the lighting path. `LE-BATTERY-CONTROL` is the separate EPCS child for the owner-built BatteryAssembly and vehicle-host BatterySupervision. Debugger inspection of operational variables is an engineering method outside this runtime decomposition. Detailed electrical realization, protection, mechanical and handling boundaries remain in ARCH-002. BMS/cell charge-acceptance facts constrain generated wheel energy entering the installed pack.

Validation is structural and source-contract review only; this Draft does not claim a SysML compiler result.
