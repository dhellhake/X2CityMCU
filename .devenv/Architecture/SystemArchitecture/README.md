# X2City vehicle system architecture SysML v2 model

`ARCH-001_System_Architecture.md` is the logical vehicle architecture authority. Load the `.sysml` resources in `Modelling/` recursively, including subfolders, as one SysML v2 model, rooted at `X2CitySystemArchitecture`.

Component navigation: [Accelerator position identification](Modelling/AcceleratorPositionIdentification/README.md), [Mechanical brake detection](Modelling/MechanicalBrakeDetection/README.md), [HMIControl](Modelling/HMIControl/README.md), [ComputeHardware requirements](Modelling/ComputeHardware_Requirements.md), [LightControl](Modelling/LightControl/README.md), and [BatteryControl](Modelling/BatteryControl/README.md).

The model is static and logical. It defines vehicle component composition and information contracts `IF-A-001` through `IF-A-008`, `IF-A-009` and the physical-energy boundary `IF-A-010`. System interfaces model required energy, signal and data exchanges plus fixed constraints; [ARCH-002](../ARCH-002_Hardware_Architecture.md) owns conductors, pins, ground, voltage classes and connector realization. `LE-ACCELERATOR` is the Draft end-to-end accelerator function: RiderDevices electrical signal through logical signal measurement and position processing to one qualified `AcceleratorPosition`, with typed policy and platform-health routes. `LE-MECHANICAL-BRAKE` is the parallel brake function: RiderDevices coded electrical signal through hardware response measurement and software state identification to one qualified `BrakeLeverState`; Session, Demand, and Light consume the composite, while Platform receives its Qualification projection. `LE-MOTOR-POSITION` supplies one complete `MotorPosition` (`Position` plus `Qualification`) to MotorControl, Platform, and Session; no separate Qualification-only transmission is defined, and qualification-only changes retain the actual position evidence time. `LE-INPUT` retains temperature and supply measurement/qualification only; it does not carry HMI settings or BMS information. `LE-HMI-CONTROL` is the EPCS sibling of riding policy: its grouped VD18MT connection carries display power, raw bytes and the supplied raw power-lock signal. Its electrical leaf conditions that raw signal to a distinct MCU-safe controller-wake signal and forwards it through HMIControl to Compute. The wake route is hardware-level and works while the CPU is asleep; UART and software execution are not prerequisites. EnergyPath makes display supply available before MCU startup. HMI software owns protocol/timestamps and exchanges qualified settings, fault, SOC, current and motion information. The accelerator and brake leaves describe HW/SW responsibility; acquisition and transfer realization remains prospective. `LE-LIGHT-CONTROL` receives the qualified normal request, mechanical brake-lever state, actual electrical-braking state and external `vehicleElectricalPowerIn`; its `LightModeSelection` and `LightActuation` children use the Front Off/On and Rear Off/Dim/Full contract. `LightActuation` contains `LightPower`, which provides `lightingPowerOut`; `LE-ENERGY-PATH` distributes `vehicleElectricalPowerOut` into the lighting path and makes display supply available before MCU startup. `LE-BATTERY-CONTROL` is the separate EPCS child for the owner-built BatteryAssembly and vehicle-host BatterySupervision. Debugger inspection of operational variables is an engineering method outside this runtime decomposition. Detailed electrical realization, protection, mechanical and handling boundaries remain in ARCH-002. BMS/cell charge-acceptance facts constrain generated wheel energy entering the installed pack.

Validation is structural and source-contract review only; this Draft does not claim a SysML compiler result.
