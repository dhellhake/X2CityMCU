# ARCH-002 — Hardware architecture and physical allocation

**Draft EPCS baseline — 2026-10-06.** Hardware ownership is limited to electrical/electronic retrofit assemblies and their actual electrical interfaces. The selected BMW X2City chassis, brakes, carrier, battery bay, locks, structural mounts and enclosures are supplied external platform givens under [ID-001 §2.1](../Requirements/ID-001_Item_Definition.md#external-mechanical-platform-assumption); this project does not decompose or qualify those mechanics or claim OEM fitness for an altered envelope. The supplied pack charge-acceptance boundary remains applicable.
**Historical Draft 1.4 — 2026-09-21 (superseded).** The earlier refinement selected the DRV8300DRGE-EVM board, WeAct STM32H723VGT6 host and Hall-required position-estimation/FOC allocation. The 2026-10-06 EPCS scope below supersedes its whole-vehicle/mechanical allocation language; electrical implementation and physical acceptance remain open.

## Scope and interpretation

The project hardware model has one root assembly, **HC-VEH-EPCS**, plus one supplied electrical pack context, **HC-PACK**. HC-PACK denotes the single 14S5P battery electrical assembly across fitted and removed configurations. BMW bay fit, retention, mounting and access geometry are external givens; only the electrical mating boundary is allocated here.

An assembly row may contain boards, real electrical wiring, connectors, protection and thermal contributors needed to realize its stated electrical boundary. Mechanical fasteners, support structure, routing geometry and enclosure design are outside project ownership. It does not select a discrete component, connector pinout, isolation topology, fuse, sensor, threshold, diagnostic coverage or timing bound. Human removal/refitting and service procedures are external collaborators, not hardware components.

HC-CELLS and HC-BMS remain supplied/fixed integration boundaries. HC-BMS includes its manufacturer firmware (`SC-BMS`); this document neither decomposes nor changes it. The DRV8300DRGE-EVM is the selected fixed board baseline for `HC-TRACTION-POWER`, subject to the project [integration and requirements-fit record](DRV8300DRGE-EVM/DRV8300DRGE-EVM_Integration_and_Requirements_Fit.md) and [allocated traction HSI](DRV8300DRGE-EVM/DRV8300DRGE-EVM_WeAct_STM32H723VGT6_Traction_HSI.md). The WeAct STM32H723VGT6 is selected as `HC-CONTROLLER` under its [allocated traction HSI](DRV8300DRGE-EVM/DRV8300DRGE-EVM_WeAct_STM32H723VGT6_Traction_HSI.md); it does not establish resource, power or physical acceptance.

### VD18MT physical refinement

The [hardware model](HardwareArchitecture/Modelling/Vd18mtElectricalConnection.sysml) defines the physical pin-level boundary and an `IfA006Vd18mtElectricalConnection` interface instance between explicit HC-VEH-HARNESS and supplied HC-RIDER connection endpoints. It imports the raw `HmiPowerLockSignal` from [HmiElectricalInterface.sysml](SystemArchitecture/Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/HMIControl/Decomposition/HmiElectricalInterface.sysml) for pin 5. `Vd18mtElectricalConnectionContext` is a connection-modeling context only; it adds no selected component and changes no containment. HC-VEH-HARNESS carries the vehicle-side electrical conductors; HC-RIDER remains the supplied display assembly. Existing HC-AUX-POWER/HC-ENERGY-DIST allocation supplies P+ to the display boundary; HC-INPUT-FE acquires its UART receive wire and conditions pin-5 switched voltage to a compatible HC-CONTROLLER wake input, including while the MCU sleeps. HC-CONTROLLER supplies transmitted UART bytes and consumes received data. The conditioned wake path is a distinct electrical refinement; the pin-level model does not assert a raw direct wire to the MCU or select a circuit, voltage or pin. The five physical wires remain unchanged.

| Logical HMI contract (`HmiDevicePort` on `vd18mtConnection` in [HmiElectricalInterface](SystemArchitecture/Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/HMIControl/Decomposition/HmiElectricalInterface.sysml)) | Physical realization in `IfA006Vd18mtElectricalConnection` |
|---|---|
| `displayPower:VehicleElectricalPower` | Pin 3 yellow P+ supply and pin 1 black ground return |
| `informationToDisplay:HmiControllerReportData` | Pin 2 green controller TX to display RX, referenced to pin 1 ground |
| `informationFromDisplay:HmiDisplayRequestData` | Pin 4 white display TX to controller RX, referenced to pin 1 ground |

The pin-5 wake refinement is `HC-RIDER` → HC-VEH-HARNESS raw pin-5 conductor → HC-INPUT-FE conditioning → compatible HC-CONTROLLER wake input. At the logical layer, `HmiElectricalInterface` publishes `HmiPowerLockObservation` (Active/Inactive/Unknown plus integrity) from raw `HmiPowerLockSignal` and separately provides the conditioned `ControllerWakeSignal`; HmiInformationProcessing interprets the observation into the qualified operation request. See [REQ-SYS-HMIE-002](SystemArchitecture/Modelling/DriverInteraction/Decomposition/HMIControl/Decomposition/HmiElectricalInterface_Requirements.md#req-sys-hmie-002) and [ComputeHardware requirements](SystemArchitecture/Modelling/Compute/Decomposition/ComputeHardware_Requirements.md). The conditioned hardware wake supports the confirmed wake function from selected deep sleep; it does not confirm the electrical Active/Inactive mapping, nor grant drive permission. The full common startup/Ready guard remains required. Mode, eligible input, electrical thresholds, loading, protection, leakage, unpowered behavior, clock/context/timebase restoration and finite wake acceptance remain open.

This is a documented trace/refinement mapping, not a formal realization proof. Bytes and vehicle-local source, validity, freshness and integrity qualification are realized by UART acquisition and controller-side processing; they are not metadata transmitted on the physical wires. The model records the known pin/color mapping and direction only. Exact connector identity, harness routing details, electrical limits, thresholds, circuits, protection and MCU pins remain unselected. Acceptance still requires characterization of the supplied display behavior, electrical levels and references, power availability and protection, UART unpowered/backfeed behavior, signal integrity, and applicable timing/qualification.

## Hosts, state and physical protection boundaries

HC-CONTROLLER is the shared EPCS host for supervision and traction. The [runtime integration contract](SoftwareArchitecture/Runtime_Integration_Contract.md) assigns project startup/IRQ/DMA bindings within this host; it does not turn an OS task or generic driver module into a logical component.

| State / action | Physical owner | Required separation |
|---|---|---|
| Riding Ready, riding fault history, rider command and traction execution | HC-CONTROLLER with vehicle physical contributors | A vehicle-controller restart starts ineligible and has no old fault history; fresh qualification is required.  |
| Cell-path backstop, balancing and vendor observations | HC-BMS | BMS trip/recovery/telemetry return is not a vehicle policy reset. Its qualified configuration and actual behavior remain supplied-component acceptance work. |

The following are allocation boundaries, not completeness or independence claims:

- **Pack-local:** HC-CELLS, HC-BMS and HC-PACK-IF define the supplied stored-energy and electrical interface boundary. BMS protection is a backstop, not commanded-current regulation or proof of all fault coverage.
- **Vehicle-local:** HC-ENERGY-DIST, HC-TRACTION-POWER, HC-AUX-POWER and HC-VEH-HARNESS must provide the eventual assigned response for vehicle energy, residual energy and wheel generation, including unavailable controller/supply/UART states.
- **Electrical accessible and service boundary:** Electrical interfaces and HC-VEH-HARNESS must be assessed through fitted, disconnected, removed-pack and service power states. A dark HMI, UART response, FET status or zero command does not demonstrate safe electrical exposure or isolation.

## Allocation limits and completion work

This architecture baseline introduces no new system requirement or hardware leaf below the stated assemblies. In particular, it selects neither a new sensor/interlock, an immutable electronic pack identity, normal BMS parameter-write capability, direct BMS J5 exposure, UART isolation implementation, current/voltage/thermal setpoint, fuse/protection circuit, torque controller circuit or claimed diagnostic coverage.

Before physical allocation acceptance, complete the existing `OI-040`–`049`, `OI-057`, `OI-061`–`063` and applicable `WS-OI` evidence: actual BMS identity/settings/UART electrical behavior; pack/vehicle power, reference and connector arrangement; all-load/storage budget; generated/residual energy and accessible-interface response; host reset behavior; electrical wiring realization, thermal limits, accessible-energy boundaries and service acceptance; and shared HC-CONTROLLER timing/resource/common-cause analysis. [BAT-001](../Battery/BAT-001_Selected_Pack_and_BMS.md), [battery integration](SystemArchitecture/Modelling/BatteryControl/Battery_Integration.md), [energy protection](../Requirements/System_Requirements/Energy_and_Protection.md), and ARCH-001 remain controlling.
