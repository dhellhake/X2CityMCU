# DD-001 — Software unit design: AcceleratorInterface

## Input, traction and platform derivation gates

`U-ACCELERATOR-QUALIFY` alone converts completed regular scans into the composite `AcceleratorPosition` record on the sole `acceleratorPositionOut` port. It has exactly `Position` (percent/rest and provenance) and `Qualification` (position qualification plus current health and diagnostic reason/severity/protection request). Producer/reset/configuration/health failure invalidates the result and never substitutes a prior value. Qualification updates may publish without a new ADC sample and remain usable for diagnosis/protection when Position is invalid; such an update preserves the Position sample provenance/age, and refreshing the publication/context envelope never refreshes measurement freshness. The inherited envelope qualifies publication/context/health evidence only and never proves Position valid. The cross-component consumer statement is retained in [shared DD-001](../../DetailedDesign/DD-001_Software_Unit_Design.md#input-traction-and-platform-derivation-gates).
