# DRV8300DRGE-EVM ↔ WeAct STM32H723VGT6 traction HSI

**Draft 0.4 — 2026-09-21.** This is the canonical, allocated vehicle traction interconnection and software HSI for `HC-TRACTION-POWER`, `HC-CONTROLLER`, `HC-INPUT-FE` and the logical traction-control responsibility. It refines the selected EVM and WeAct integration records; it does not alter an approved requirement, authorize firmware, or demonstrate bench or vehicle acceptance. The detached `HC-CHARGE-HOST` is a separate host domain. Its physical controller remains open and consumes none of the following vehicle pins.

## Allocation basis and fixed exclusions

The allocation uses the LQFP100 STM32H723VG functions in ST DS13313 Rev. 5 and the WeAct V1.2 header record. `TIM8` provides the six complementary inputs and its break input; 32-bit `TIM2` timestamps all three Hall edges against one counter. `PA0..PA5` and `PC0..PC5` are the twelve analogue pins. These pins are physically brought out on P2 except TIM8 high-side `PC6..PC8` on P1. This avoids the `PB0` PWM/ADC collision.

`PA9/PA10` remain the present USART1 debug console at 115200 8N1; no firmware change is made by this record. `USART3` `PD8/PD9` AF7 is reserved for the protected BMS UART, and `UART4` `PC10/PC11` AF8 is reserved for the VD18MT UART after its separate level/protection qualification. SWD .`PA13/PA14`), HSE, reset, USB, fitted LCD, its SPI4 pins, and fitted OSPI pins .including PB6) are unavailable. The vehicle configuration prohibits a microSD card and SDMMC1 because PC8 is traction PWM and PC10/PC11 are UART4; verify socket/card absence and no SD network loading. Camera use is **not permitted in the vehicle configuration**: no camera module/FPC may be fitted or electrically drive `PC6`, `PC7`, or `PA7`; the adapter acceptance records a no-camera inspection, isolation and continuity result. This is a controlled loss of camera function, not an inference from an unused header. The unused camera connector does not make any other fixed-board loading safe.

## Physical harness and logic contract

All EVM signal returns terminate at the adapter analogue/digital return point and then at WeAct P2-15/P2-43 or P2-44 as appropriate; the exact harness shall use paired signal/return conductors for current/voltage observations and Hall. Do not use a power return as an arbitrary signal return. Every MCU-facing interface is 3.3 V. The adapter shall protect each input against expected fault and unpowered paths, retain ADC source impedance/settling evidence, and shall not apply 5 V to an MCU pin.

## Independent inhibit, analogue and power states

The adapter implements six 3.3-V AND/isolation channels after the MCU series resistors. Their common `PERMIT` input is normally low .including adapter power-up, MCU reset, MCU loss and fault-latch reset). `PERMIT` may become high only with the independent hardware supervisor healthy, its latched analogue fault clear and an MCU run-enable request; an asserted fault or supervisor/heartbeat loss forces it low without firmware execution. `DRV_INHIBIT_N` is the active-low, open-drain fault output of that supervisor, reaches PA6 and is wired in parallel to force the six EVM PWM inputs low. Required independent analogue inputs are three shunt-current comparators, a DC-link overvoltage comparator, a qualified board-temperature fault path and the external hardware/watchdog fault condition. Thresholds, response, fault coverage, component ratings, latch-reset conditions and generated-energy disposition are **not yet qualified**. This selected topology is required because EVM `FAULT` is LED-only and the driver has no credited fault output.

At POR/reset, configure PWM pins as GPIO inputs with no pull-up and keep `PERMIT=0`; do not enable alternate functions, `MOE`, ADC-trigger output, or run-enable until the current configuration identity, calibrated offsets, Hall map, current authority, fresh acquisition and hardware-permit observation are all qualified. On any reset/brownout/watchdog/DMAMUX/DMA/ADC/timer fault, first withdraw run-enable and `MOE`, invalidate the epoch and discard outstanding DMA data. Deasserting PWM does not prove torque, energy isolation or safe DC-link voltage. The external 5-V Hall source is independently current-limited and shall remain off until its unpowered-host/backfeed behavior is accepted; the EVM does not generate that 5-V supply.

## Timer, ADC, DMA and clock configuration

The initial engineering configuration is intentionally quantitative so it can be reproduced and tested, but its values are limits to qualify rather than approved vehicle ratings.

| Resource | Selected configuration |
|---|---|
| clocks | 25-MHz HSE; current firmware profile: SYSCLK 550 MHz, HCLK 275 MHz, APB2 137.5 MHz. With APB2 prescaled and `TIMPRE=0`, `TIM8CLK=275 MHz`. ADC kernel clock is asynchronous PLL2P=15 MHz, generated by HSE/5×96/32; `ADC_CCR.CKMODE=00`, `PRESC=/1`, `BOOST=10` and optimized 12-bit `RES=110`. This conservative clock meets the 12-bit slow-channel rating before the final VDDA/input-class qualification. Do not derive ADC clock from APB2.
| PWM | TIM8 centre-aligned mode 3, PSC=0, ARR=6875, RCR=1, CH1/2/3 and complementary CH1N/2N/3N enabled only under `MOE`/permit. RM0468's centre-aligned endpoint sequence gives `fPWM=275,000,000/.2×6875)=20,000 Hz`. The normative startup sequence is `MOE=0`, `CEN=0`; configure CMS3/PSC0/ARR6875/RCR1, ARPE and OC1..4PE; write initial preloads; issue `UG` to load shadows/internal repetition count; clear UIF; force/readback `CNT=0` and upward direction; then set `CEN=1`. The expected first UEV is underflow and then one UEV per 50 µs. Target readback/scope verification before energization shall confirm this phase; mismatch makes acquisition/output unavailable, not an alternate accepted phase. | ADC trigger | TIM8 CH4 is internal-only, PWM mode 2; `TIM8_CR2.MMS2=0111` exports OC4REF as a TRGO2 level and ADC1 `JEXTEN=01` selects its rising edge. This yields one injected trigger per complete up/down carrier, rather than both compare crossings. CH4 output remains disabled. `CCR4` is a preload value computed one carrier ahead with `CCR1..3` for the next commanded SVPWM/voltage-vector sector and duty pattern's proven rank-1 common-low-side window; a Hall sector supplies rotor angle but does not establish conducting shunts. Enforce `1<CCR4<ARR-1` and do not make an abrupt `ARR-1→ARR/1→0` transition. The commissioning evidence defines the allowable `CCR4` region by commanded vector sector, duty and modulation; no admissible region means torque unavailable.
| fast ADC | ADC1 master and ADC2 slave, `ADC12_COMMON.CCR.DUAL=00101` .dual injected simultaneous), timer-triggered, non-continuous, optimized 12-bit, two injected ranks each, equal sequence length. The commanded-vector-selected direct context is: A/B: ADC1 `[ISENA,ISENC]`, ADC2 `[ISENB,SEN_PVDD]`; B/C: ADC1 `[ISENB,ISENA]`, ADC2 `[ISENC,SEN_PVDD]`; C/A: ADC1 `[ISENC,ISENB]`, ADC2 `[ISENA,SEN_PVDD]`. With SMP=64.5 cycles and 15-MHz ADC clock, two ranks including maximum trigger latency take at most 145.5 cycles = 9.700 µs. An in-progress regular conversion is interrupted by the injected event and resumes after its injected group; account for the 3.5-cycle `tLATRINJ` worst case. `PCSEL` preselects channels 0–3 and SMPR remains 64.5 cycles for all while armed. `JQDIS=1`; the context queue is disabled. The FOC reconstruction uses only the same-aperture rank-1 pair. The modulation limiter must prove that commanded vector's pair is valid at the selected `CCR4`; otherwise torque is unavailable.

| Hall | TIM2 PSC=274, 32-bit 1-MHz counter, ARR=`0xFFFF_FFFF`, CH1/3/4 both-edge input capture. Ring identity gives channel. A static state is independently sampled; absent edge at rest is not itself a fault. |
| priorities | TIM8 break: highest maskable priority; ADC1/ADC2 JEOS: next; ADC/regular-DMA error: same critical class; TIM8 update/latch: next; TIM2 Hall capture DMA: below fast loop; planned nominal regular DMA and communications below. The proposed deadline guard is: for every allowed `CCR4`, hardware JEOS assertion occurs no later than trigger+9.700 µs; measured CPU IRQ observation, stop/poll/readback/rearm and `CCR1..4` preload writes must finish before the selected next underflow UEV by a configured margin proved under worst-case ISR/bus contention. The bounded checked PRIMASK scopes defer all configurable software IRQ activation, including break service, so their measured maximum duration is part of the break-IRQ latency and next-underflow budget. TIM8 break hardware and the independent inhibit withdraw physical PWM permit during that deferral. No ISR may block, allocate, log synchronously or take an unbounded lock in the fast budget. Missing the margin keeps the master disarmed, invalidates the next epoch, and withdraws MOE/run-enable before any later partial compare use.

With the 15-MHz selected ADC clock, ADC1/ADC2 offset calibration takes 1280 ADC cycles .85.333 µs) and full offset-plus-linearity calibration takes 165010 cycles .11.001 ms). Initialization shall verify the hardware completion indication rather than delay alone; its timeout shall exceed the applicable value and retain `PERMIT=0`, `MOE=0` and acquisition unavailable on expiry.

### Regular ADC configuration derivation gate

The 1-kHz regular scan is a planned nominal integration target, not a selected STM32 trigger or rank table. Before implementation, the platform responsibility shall derive and record the regular trigger source, start phase, rate/jitter bound, ADC1 and ADC2 rank/channel tables, DMA scan lengths, injected-pre-emption behavior and error/vector binding in the [DEC-001 decision record].../../../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md). At startup it shall read back those identities and hold regular acquisition unavailable on absent, mismatched or unqualified configuration. Measurement must establish the achieved period/jitter and the independent 10-ms consumer latency; neither nominal value is acceptance evidence.

### Applied winding-voltage evidence gate

The physical applied-winding-voltage source is distinct from the System `MotorPhaseElectrical` contract. Applied winding-voltage evidence is initially unavailable and requires a qualified physical source and hardware observation. That design shall qualify phase A/B/C values; PWM-average reference and frame; application/observation time; output-stage and configuration provenance; bandwidth; current-to-voltage and phase-to-phase skew; uncertainty/error bounds; and invalidation on unavailable or mismatched source/configuration. The regular `VSENA/B/C` scan remains regular phase-voltage diagnostic information and is excluded from this path. The current HSI does not select an observer algorithm, a reconstruction formula, peripheral resource or timing acceptance for this input.

### Resource ownership and acquisition validity

The platform responsibility shall establish and validate peripheral, rank, trigger, vector, priority and D2-resource configuration. Acquisition and traction responsibilities shall validate applicable calibration, Hall mapping and configuration while permit and MOE are low. Changes and resets must invalidate affected acquisition context; unavailable or mismatched configuration must prevent torque availability.

Active DMA regions are non-cacheable and exclusively owned by DMA. A consumer must establish that a completed circular half remains stable throughout a bounded copy; interrupt masking cannot prevent DMA overwrite. Missed completion, ambiguous writer window, error or exhausted capacity makes the data unavailable. Buffer depth, maximum blocking and copy time, freshness and accelerator/brake end-to-end latency remain validation gates.

Record STM32 revision ID at boot and apply ES0491 ADC 2.10.1/2.10.2 restrictions to both A and Z revisions with `JQDIS=1`. No JSQR/SMPR/PCSEL write is permitted while either converter is armed. Disarm and verify both converters before updating injected context, with slave JSQR written and checked before master JSQR; master `JL=1`, `JEXTSEL=01010` and `JEXTEN=01`, with the slave trigger disabled or ignored. A missed rearm/readback deadline invalidates the next sample and withdraws torque. ADC 2.10.5/2.10.11 require JEOS identity, range and plausibility checks and a calibrated cross-ADC error budget. An unreviewed silicon revision makes fast acquisition unavailable.

Fast feedback requires complete, fresh, coherent samples, qualified windows and configuration, and valid ADC, DMA, timer, break and inhibit status. Missing completion, excessive skew, deadline miss or fault makes affected feedback unavailable and withdraws torque under the existing fault and session rules. Vdc has rank-2 skew and is not simultaneous with the rank-1 current pair. Physical output and energy-path observations remain distinct from commands.

## Acceptance limits and trace

Before energization, demonstrate on the actual assembly: connector/J3/rail continuity; PWM inactive state across every reset/boot/watchdog sequence; no 5-V injection/backfeed; permit/inhibit response independent of MCU; effective FET dead time; ADC input range/source settling/reference error; trigger aperture/latency and next-latch deadline; JEOS/regular-DMA/cache error injection; current offset/gain/polarity and phase/Hall map; valid low-side sample windows over the proposed modulation/current envelope; Hall threshold and timing; hardware comparator/latch coverage; board/motor temperature paths; Vdc transient/generated-energy behavior; physical-output/energy observation; and, before enabling observer assistance, the separate applied-winding-voltage source/provenance, timing, bandwidth/skew and error qualification. The initial 20-kHz, 64.5-cycle sample-time and 15-MHz ADC-clock design targets are engineering configuration limits, not proven EVM, thermal, current, torque, 40-km/h or safety limits.

This HSI refines `HW-I-003`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-001/004/007`, `REQ-SYS-TRQHW-001`, `REQ-SYS-MSM-001/002`, `REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001` and `REQ-SYS-HSI-009`. The physical Hall/current branches retain `REQ-SYS-TRQHW-002` and `REQ-SYS-MSM-001/002`.
