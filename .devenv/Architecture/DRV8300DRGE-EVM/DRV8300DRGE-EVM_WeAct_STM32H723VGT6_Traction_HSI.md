# DRV8300DRGE-EVM ↔ WeAct STM32H723VGT6 traction HSI

It refines the selected EVM and WeAct integration records; it does not alter an approved requirement, authorize firmware, or demonstrate bench or vehicle acceptance. The detached `HC-CHARGE-HOST` is a separate host domain. Its physical controller remains open and consumes none of the following vehicle pins.

## Allocation basis and fixed exclusions

The allocation uses the LQFP100 STM32H723VG functions in ST DS13313 Rev. 5 and the WeAct V1.2 header record. `TIM8` provides the six complementary inputs and its break input; 32-bit `TIM2` timestamps all three Hall edges against one counter. `PA0..PA5` and `PC0..PC5` are the twelve analogue pins. These pins are physically brought out on P2 except TIM8 high-side `PC6..PC8` on P1. This avoids the `PB0` PWM/ADC collision.

`PA9/PA10` remain the present USART1 debug console at 115200 8N1; no firmware change is made by this record. `USART3` `PD8/PD9` AF7 is reserved for the protected BMS UART, and `UART4` `PC10/PC11` AF8 is reserved for the VD18MT UART after its separate level/protection qualification. SWD .`PA13/PA14`), HSE, reset, USB, fitted LCD, its SPI4 pins, and fitted OSPI pins .including PB6) are unavailable. The vehicle configuration prohibits a microSD card and SDMMC1 because PC8 is traction PWM and PC10/PC11 are UART4; verify socket/card absence and no SD network loading. Camera use is **not permitted in the vehicle configuration**: no camera module/FPC may be fitted or electrically drive `PC6`, `PC7`, or `PA7`; the adapter acceptance records a no-camera inspection, isolation and continuity result. This is a controlled loss of camera function, not an inference from an unused header. The unused camera connector does not make any other fixed-board loading safe.

## Independent inhibit, analogue and power states

The adapter implements six 3.3-V AND/isolation channels after the MCU series resistors. Their common `PERMIT` input is normally low .including adapter power-up, MCU reset, MCU loss and fault-latch reset). `PERMIT` may become high only with the independent hardware supervisor healthy, its latched analogue fault clear and an MCU run-enable request; an asserted fault or supervisor/heartbeat loss forces it low without firmware execution. `DRV_INHIBIT_N` is the active-low, open-drain fault output of that supervisor, reaches PA6 and is wired in parallel to force the six EVM PWM inputs low. Required independent analogue inputs are three shunt-current comparators, a DC-link overvoltage comparator, a qualified board-temperature fault path and the external hardware/watchdog fault condition. Thresholds, response, fault coverage, component ratings, latch-reset conditions and generated-energy disposition are **not yet qualified**. This selected topology is required because EVM `FAULT` is LED-only and the driver has no credited fault output.

At POR/reset, configure PWM pins as GPIO inputs with no pull-up and keep `PERMIT=0`; do not enable alternate functions, `MOE`, ADC-trigger output, or run-enable until the current configuration identity, calibrated offsets, Hall map, current authority, fresh acquisition and hardware-permit observation are all qualified. On any reset/brownout/watchdog/DMAMUX/DMA/ADC/timer fault, first withdraw run-enable and `MOE`, invalidate the epoch and discard outstanding DMA data. Deasserting PWM does not prove torque, energy isolation or safe DC-link voltage. The external 5-V Hall source is independently current-limited and shall remain off until its unpowered-host/backfeed behavior is accepted; the EVM does not generate that 5-V supply.

### Regular ADC configuration derivation gate

The 1-kHz regular scan is a planned nominal integration target, not a selected STM32 trigger or rank table. Before implementation, `U-PLATFORM-BINDING` shall derive and record the regular trigger source, start phase, rate/jitter bound, ADC1 and ADC2 rank/channel tables, DMA scan lengths, injected-pre-emption behavior and error/vector binding in the [DEC-001 decision record].../../../Requirements/DEC-001_Decisions_and_Open_Issues.md). At startup it shall read back those identities and hold regular acquisition unavailable on absent, mismatched or unqualified configuration. Measurement must establish the achieved period/jitter and the independent 10-ms consumer latency; neither nominal value is acceptance evidence.

### Detailed unit ownership and record lifetime

`U-PLATFORM-CONTEXT` creates operation/configuration contexts. `U-PLATFORM-CALIBRATION` alone validates canonical immutable `CalibrationBundle` while permit/MOE are low, including raw-acquisition/rank-trigger, current/phase, supply, Hall, accelerator, brake and temperature parameter families; it rolls context, invalidates every dependent raw/derived/command record and atomically activates the complete bundle without live mutation. Bundle ID/revision/context bind the payload; they are not calibration data. `U-PLATFORM-BINDING` applies static peripheral, rank, trigger, vector, priority and D2-resource setup only under that active bundle. `U-ANALOG-ACQ-FAST` alone snapshots raw JEOS/JDR data; `U-MOTOR-PHASE-FAST` owns current scale/offset/polarity, valid-window check and reconstruction; `U-SUPPLY-FAST` owns calibrated fast Vdc from rank 2; `U-HALL-POSITION` owns the complete electrical-position result. `U-TRACTION-CONTROL` consumes those results and plans the next context, while `U-TRACTION-OUTPUT` is the sole dynamic `CCR1..4` and JSQR writer.

Regular and Hall DMA IRQs only record completion/error metadata. A consumer accepts a circular half only after a hardware `NDTR`/write-window snapshot proves DMA is writing the other half; it makes a bounded volatile/raw copy to CPU-owned immutable storage, then verifies writer window and DMA status again. Maximum IRQ/task blocking plus this copy must be less than a half recurrence, preventing an undetected writer traversal and return; missed completion, ambiguous window, error or exhausted capacity makes the record unavailable. Masking CPU interrupts cannot stop DMA overwrite, so an atomic index/read/re-read sequence alone is forbidden. The active D2 DMA region is non-cacheable and cannot have a Rust mutable CPU borrow while DMA owns it; a separately CPU-owned snapshot has synchronised atomic publication or a proved lock. The 1-kHz regular producer is a planned nominal target until the required trigger/rank derivation/readback passes; 10 ms is only the proposed consumer period because deployment has 5/10-ms work and no DMA wakeup. Its buffer depth, maximum blocking/copy, freshness and accelerator/brake end-to-end latency are validation gates.

`U-PLATFORM-CONTEXT` orchestrates startup/context invalidation. `U-PLATFORM-BINDING` owns static resource/rank/trigger/vector binding and checked resource wrappers; it dispatches JEOS to `U-ANALOG-ACQ-FAST`, which makes the bounded raw JDR/status/timer snapshot. `U-MOTOR-PHASE-FAST` and `U-SUPPLY-FAST` derive feedback, `U-HALL-POSITION` supplies the complete position, and `U-TRACTION-CONTROL` produces `PWMAndADCSamplingPlan`. `U-TRACTION-OUTPUT` alone performs the guarded `JADSTP`/disarm/readback/UDIS/CCR1..4/slave-then-master-JSQR/rearm/closing-barrier transaction; it contains no loop, wait, FOC, logging, allocation, MOE/RUN_EN assertion, `UG` or later enable write. Break hardware and external inhibit withdraw permit during a short masked commit; after unmask the fault path latches, invalidates all records/context, writes literal RUN_EN-low/MOE-clear images, sets UDIS, stops injected conversion and completes the barrier. Only a fresh clean-restart operation generation clears a recognised latched fault; startup uncertainty is distinct. Checked token access, mask duration, break latency, MMIO/barrier ordering and fault injection remain qualification gates. Two complete current-context records are required before fast input becomes usable.

Errata policy is part of the configuration identity: record STM32 revision ID at boot; apply ES0491 ADC 2.10.1/2.10.2 restrictions to both A and Z revisions by holding `JQDIS=1` and disabling the context queue. After the JDR/status snapshot, issue master `JADSTP`, wait for master and slave `JADSTART=0`, then write/readback slave JSQR first and master JSQR last .`JL=1`; master `JEXTSEL=01010` TIM8_TRGO2 and `JEXTEN=01`; slave trigger disabled/ignored), and arm master only through the UDIS-guarded commit sequence. No JSQR/SMPR/PCSEL write is permitted while either converter is armed. A missed rearm/readback deadline invalidates the next sample and withdraws torque; the master remains disarmed, preventing a mixed context. ADC 2.10.5/2.10.11 require the JEOS identity/range/plausibility checks above plus a calibrated cross-ADC error budget. A silicon revision not covered by the recorded errata review makes fast acquisition unavailable until reviewed; it is not silently treated as equivalent.

## Epoch record, arithmetic and software ownership

`U-ANALOG-ACQ-FAST` publishes the following raw immutable `InjectedEpoch` only after reading ADC1/ADC2 JDR1/JDR2 and both JEOS/status. `SC-ANALOG-ACQ.V` owns raw regular and injected records, `SC-HALL-IF.V` owns Hall capture through complete electrical position, and traction owns acceptance/control only. No component infers physical output from a command.

```text
InjectedEpoch {
  u32 configuration_id; u32 reset_context; u32 pwm_epoch; u32 adc_context_id;
  u32 acquisition_sequence;
  u32 tim8_trigger_tick; u16 adc1_isen_a; u16 adc2_isen_b;
  u16 adc1_isen_c; u16 adc2_vdc; u16 status_bits;
  u16 rank_skew_ns; u16 aperture_ns; u16 carrier_phase; u16 reserved;
  Quality { adc_overrun, jeos_missing, window_a, window_b, window_c,
            vdc_range, saturation, deadline, stale, break_active, inhibit_active }
}
```

The JEOS writer fills the raw payload and release-publishes its sequence. It is a direct same-context handoff, not a cyclic or intercom record. `U-MOTOR-PHASE-FAST` derives calibrated `PhaseCurrentFeedbackRecord` using `Iphase=.Vadc-offset_phase)/gain_phase`; `U-SUPPLY-FAST` derives `FastDcLinkVoltageRecord` using `Vdc=Vadc×33.425` before divider/reference calibration. Vdc retains rank-2 skew and is not asserted simultaneous with the rank-1 pair. Raw codes, reference, coefficients, phase association, Hall map/alignment and timer configuration are bound into `configuration_id`; `adc_context_id` identifies the exact vector/rank mapping. A configuration change creates a new reset/acquisition context. `Iq` sign, electrical angle, phase mapping and positive wheel torque are one calibration bundle; if absent or mismatched, traction requests zero of both signs.

At each valid epoch, `U-MOTOR-PHASE-FAST` reconstructs only a qualified same-epoch pair and checks direct third-channel plausibility. `U-TRACTION-CONTROL` transforms that feedback using `U-HALL-POSITION`'s bounded electrical angle, uses `Id*=0`, applies `Iq*` only after authority/command/calibration acceptance, runs PI with vector saturation/anti-windup, and publishes `PWMAndADCSamplingPlan`: next buffered `CCR1..3`, `CCR4` sampling plan and vector ADC context. `U-TRACTION-OUTPUT` alone writes literal buffered registers in its guarded transaction. No forced alignment, start motion, hold current, sensorless fallback, field weakening or torque estimate is permitted.

The fast loop declares unavailable on a missing expected completion, a stale sequence, an incomplete first pair, any required saturation/range/window failure, skew beyond the configuration limit, a Hall/configuration/authority mismatch, deadline miss, ADC/DMA/timer error or asserted break. A missing first sample after enable is initialization state, not a newly recognised system fault. Once previously usable data fails, torque is withdrawn, diagnostic status is published and existing parent fault/session rules decide recognition and recovery. The record reports requested PWM, accepted command and hardware-permit/break observation separately from qualified physical-output and energy-path observations.

## Acceptance limits and trace

Before energization, demonstrate on the actual assembly: connector/J3/rail continuity; PWM inactive state across every reset/boot/watchdog sequence; no 5-V injection/backfeed; permit/inhibit response independent of MCU; effective FET dead time; ADC input range/source settling/reference error; trigger aperture/latency and next-latch deadline; JEOS/regular-DMA/cache error injection; current offset/gain/polarity and phase/Hall map; valid low-side sample windows over the proposed modulation/current envelope; Hall threshold and timing; hardware comparator/latch coverage; board/motor temperature paths; Vdc transient/generated-energy behavior; and physical-output/energy observation. The initial 20-kHz, 64.5-cycle sample-time and 15-MHz ADC-clock design targets are engineering configuration limits, not proven EVM, thermal, current, torque, 40-km/h or safety limits.

This HSI refines `HW-I-003`, `REQ-SYS-HSI-001/004/007`, `REQ-SYS-TRQHW-001`, `REQ-SYS-TRQCTL-001` and `UR-TRACTION-CONTROL-001`; its new Draft requirement chain is `REQ-SYS-HSI-009 → REQ-SYS-TRQHW-002 / REQ-SYS-TRQIO-001 / REQ-SYS-TRQEX-001 → UR-ANALOG-ACQ-FAST-001 / UR-MOTOR-PHASE-FAST-001 / UR-SUPPLY-FAST-001 / UR-HALL-CAPTURE-001 / UR-HALL-POSITION-001 / UR-TRACTION-OUTPUT-001`.
